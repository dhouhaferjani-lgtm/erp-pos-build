Where should Smart Prompts appear in the POS?

When a cashier adds a parapharmacy item to the cart, where do recommendations show up?

A

Inline in Cart Panel

A collapsible "Smart Prompts" section appears below the cart items, above the totals. Shows 3-5 compact recommendation cards. Cashier taps one to add it. Disappears after checkout or dismiss.

Pros

  • Visible without extra action
  • Close to the cart context
  • Easy to quick-add

Cons

  • Takes vertical space in an already-dense cart panel
  • May feel intrusive during fast checkout
B

Slide-up Toast / Bottom Bar

A subtle horizontal bar slides up at the bottom of the screen (or bottom of the product grid) showing "💡 3 suggestions available" — tapping it expands to show recommendation cards. Auto-dismisses after 10s if ignored.

Pros

  • Non-intrusive — doesn't disrupt flow
  • Cashier opts in when time permits
  • Works well on touch screens

Cons

  • Easy to miss or ignore
  • Extra tap to see details
C

Temporary Product Grid Overlay

The product grid briefly replaces its content with recommendation cards for 5-8 seconds after adding an item. Shows "Suggested for this cart" header. Tapping anywhere or waiting returns to normal grid. Cashier can tap a recommendation to add it.

Pros

  • Maximum visibility — uses the large grid area
  • Familiar card layout
  • Natural touch target size

Cons

  • Interrupts product browsing
  • Could annoy power users in high-volume mode
D

Dedicated Sidebar Tab

Add a "💡 Suggestions" tab next to the cart panel (or as a toggle within it). When recommendations exist, a badge shows the count. Cashier switches to it when consulting with a customer about complementary products.

Pros

  • Always accessible, never intrusive
  • Full space for rich recommendation info
  • Pharmacist consultation-friendly

Cons

  • Not visible by default — requires intent
  • May go unused if not discoverable

My recommendation: Option B (slide-up toast) — it's non-intrusive for fast retail checkout but surfaceable when a pharmacist is advising a customer. The parapharmacy context is consultation-heavy, not speed-checkout.
