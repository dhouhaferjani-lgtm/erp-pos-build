The IZI POS design system

One source of truth for the web dashboard, desktop app and mobile apps — anchored in the IZI POS brand mark. A universal CRM / ERP / POS, so it stays calm and legible as screens get data-dense: structure comes from hairline borders, numbers from a monospaced face, and color is spent only where it carries meaning.

Structure & Density

Everything sits on a 4px grid. Structure comes from hairline borders — cards are flat by default, so a dense screen stays quiet. Shadow is reserved for things that truly float (dropdowns, modals, toasts). Corners are tight (10px cards) and the focus ring is the one place navy gives way to orange.

10

UX Principles

The rules every screen audit is measured against. Our operator is busy, often non-technical, and frequently new. Design for their first day, not ours.

1
One primary action
Every screen has a single obvious next step in orange. Don’t make people choose between five equal buttons.
2
Plain words, no jargon
“Selling price”, not “Unit cost (gross)”. Labels and errors read like a helpful colleague talking.
3
Big, forgiving targets
44px minimum hit areas. Steppers and toggles beat tiny dropdowns on a busy counter.
4
Progressive disclosure
Adding a product asks for name & price first; tax, barcode and modifiers wait under “More options”.
5
Always confirm & undo
Saved? Say so with a toast. Destructive? Confirm. Mistakes should be one tap to reverse.
6
Consistent across platforms
Web, desktop and mobile share these tokens and patterns, so learning one teaches the others.
Delete “Flat white”?
It will be removed from the POS screen. Past sales keep their records. You can’t undo this.
