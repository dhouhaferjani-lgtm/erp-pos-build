## 6. Reading notes carried forward for the fix session

1. **The v3 blackout (T1) is one defect with eleven faces, and the data is already there.** Every projector already reads the fields the missing events need. Emit inside the existing `fiscal_event_id` idempotency guard, inside the transaction, after the write.
2. **Verification must be made honest before anything is "verified".** ES-07 and ES-08 mean the two commands an operator would run to confirm a fix are currently incapable of failing on the fiscal era. Any fix landed before those are corrected is unverifiable by construction.
3. **Rule 8 is not a blocker, it is the method.** Every gap here is closed by a **new** event (or a new consumer), never by editing or renaming a class already in use. `InstrumentCleared`→`InstrumentRepresented` (ES-56) and the `CashCountRecordedV2` fallback already sketched in the B2 ticket are the worked examples.
4. **Idempotency is not free.** `journal_entries(source_type, source_id)` has no uniqueness constraint (~40 distinct `source_type` literals in use); dedupe is a per-call-site `exists()` check. Any new event-driven posting path must carry its own guard.
5. **Section 05 is not evidence.** Ten of its findings are SUSPECTED, its dead-event detection is self-declared LOW confidence, and two of its claims are already contradicted by section 04. Re-verify before acting on ES-34, ES-76, ES-88.
