# M4 — SV-10 blind-mode leak audit

## Scope and test method

This audit follows rendered values through the production caller rather than inferring safety from component names. The acceptance question is narrow: while `require_blind_cash_count` is true and before **Commit Counts**, does any rendered surface disclose an expected-cash or variance magnitude?

The regression at `apps/pos/src/components/pos/CashReconciliationSection.test.tsx:276` enters an actual count that creates a critical `-60.00` variance, then asserts that the Expected/Variance columns, `-60.00`, reveal summary, reason prompt, and manager-PIN prompt are absent before Commit Counts and present afterward. A temporary mutation removing the `committed` guards made that test fail on the pre-commit reason prompt; restoring the guards returned it green. Round-1 review then found two parent/child bypasses that the isolated test could not see. The modal-level regression at `apps/pos/src/components/pos/EndOfDayPreviewModal.test.tsx:535-575` now covers the summary cards, VAT breakdown, CASH, electronic CARD, and physical CHECK totals across Commit Counts; the child regression at `apps/pos/src/components/pos/organisms/CashCountTable.test.tsx:75-101` covers the electronic Actual cell. Both failed against the pre-fix render output.

Round-2 review found a temporal first-open bypass while the policy request raced the local preview, plus a stale `false → true` policy risk on a later open. The modal regression at `apps/pos/src/components/pos/EndOfDayPreviewModal.test.tsx:498-533` proves neither unresolved nor unavailable policy state renders the preview. Header integration tests at `apps/pos/src/components/__tests__/Header.test.tsx:278-390` prove the modal receives an empty unresolved policy during first load and during a later false-to-true refresh. The first-open modal test failed against the pre-fix render, exposing the exact legacy expected card.

Round-3 review widened the walk beyond the active modal. It identified the cashier-accessible Today's Sales route as a cross-navigation reconstruction path, the availability cost of failing closed without a cached policy, two non-variance money rows omitted from the enumeration, a terminal-refresh race, missing locale resources, and preview-error precedence. The narrow races/i18n/error issues are fixed and rendered tests cover them. The two product-level findings are recorded in tickets and in this audit rather than being hidden by an over-broad clean claim.

Round-4 review exercised a complete `committed → policy pending → policy resolved` cycle. It found that the child count state was recreated pre-commit while the parent still considered the old policy committed, reopening the financial summary leaks. The parent flow is now bound to the exact resolved policy snapshot; an older payload, readiness value, or commit flag is invalid immediately when Header supplies a refreshed policy object. A rendered regression crosses the full cycle and proves physical tender totals and the confirm action return to their pre-commit state.

## Result

Within the active production End of Day modal, no pre-commit surface renders or exactly reconstructs expected-cash or variance magnitude under blind mode. The narrow production changes fail closed while policy resolution is pending/unavailable, bind settings/managers and cash-count state to the current terminal/policy snapshot, synchronize the Commit Counts reveal boundary into the parent modal, hide financial preview amounts until that boundary, suppress the electronic expected-value duplicate, localize the unavailable state, and preserve genuine preview errors. A transient terminal-policy refresh deliberately discards in-progress counts rather than carrying a stale commit across the trust boundary; the operator-workflow decision is recorded in the availability ticket. The changes do not alter money computation, authorization, or non-blind rendering. The existing legacy-card defence remains unchanged.

That result is intentionally not a whole-device clean claim: the cashier-accessible Today's Sales route can reconstruct expected cash on the ordinary cash-only/no-movement scenario. Its product/authorization decision and the fail-closed Z-availability trade-off are separately ticketed. The audit also enumerates the manager-only X Report, manager/demo shift screen, tolerance drill-down, and cash-rounding row so that clean or out-of-scope surfaces are explicit rather than omitted.
