# M4 — SV-10 blind-mode leak audit

## Scope and test method

This audit follows rendered values through the production caller rather than inferring safety from component names. The acceptance question is narrow: while `require_blind_cash_count` is true and before **Commit Counts**, does any rendered surface disclose an expected-cash or variance magnitude?

The regression at `apps/pos/src/components/pos/CashReconciliationSection.test.tsx:276` enters an actual count that creates a critical `-60.00` variance, then asserts that the Expected/Variance columns, `-60.00`, reveal summary, reason prompt, and manager-PIN prompt are absent before Commit Counts and present afterward. A temporary mutation removing the `committed` guards made that test fail on the pre-commit reason prompt; restoring the guards returned it green. Round-1 review then found two parent/child bypasses that the isolated test could not see. The modal-level regression at `apps/pos/src/components/pos/EndOfDayPreviewModal.test.tsx:492-526` now covers CASH, electronic CARD, and physical CHECK payment totals across Commit Counts; the child regression at `apps/pos/src/components/pos/organisms/CashCountTable.test.tsx:75-101` covers the electronic Actual cell. Both failed against the pre-fix render output.

## Result

After the round-1 fixes, no reachable cashier pre-commit surface directly renders expected-cash or variance magnitude under blind mode. The narrow production change synchronizes only the Commit Counts reveal boundary into the parent modal and suppresses the electronic expected-value duplicate; it does not alter money computation, confirmation readiness, authorization, or non-blind rendering. The existing legacy-card defence remains unchanged. M4 also records the separate manager/demo screen for follow-up rather than expanding this lane into a close-screen redesign.
