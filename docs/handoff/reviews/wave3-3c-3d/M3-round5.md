## Register

**1. P2 — CONFIRMED — `apps/api/app/Modules/Accounting/Presentation/Console/CheckCogsCoverageCommand.php:297-305` — D-f's POS anti-join is keyed at product grain, so a sibling line on the *same* product masks the missing variant movement that round 4 exists to expose.**
The `whereNotExists` correlates on `reference_id` / `product_id` / `tenant_id` / `company_id` / `reference_type` only — **no `variant_id`** — while `writeLines` (`:1278`) writes one `pos_receipt_lines` row per canonical line item with no dedup (`originalPosSaleBasis`'s own docblock, `:2435-2438`, states duplicate product lines are normal). `decrementStock` (`:1962-1978`) writes one movement per line, so a receipt with two variant lines of the same product where only one variant grain exists produces exactly one movement — and that single row satisfies the identical correlated subquery for **both** lines. The unseeded-variant line is classified `stock_movement_expected = true` (`:1258-1259`) and then not reported.
*Failure scenario:* the launch parapharmacy sells two sizes of one SKU in one basket; the second size's variant grain was never seeded. Revenue books, that size's stock is untouched, COGS is zero, and D-f is silent because the first size's movement covers the tuple. This is precisely the population round-3 finding 2 blocked on.
*Why it matters at the gate rather than as a note:* the milestone's own artifacts state the guarantee unconditionally — `M3-evidence.md` ("the locked writer runs … and D-f continues to report the missing movement") and release-note §2 (`docs/follow-ups/2026-08-10-dpa-wave3-cogs-at-exit-release-note.md:55-57`, "A missing variant-scoped grain remains an anomaly: the writer warns and D-f reports it"). No test constructs a two-line same-product receipt, so nothing distinguishes the covered case from the masked one. The M3 evidence-contract row (`brief:542`) names exactly this anti-pattern.
*Mitigating and in the implementer's favour:* the brief transmits the anti-join key literally as `(reference_type, reference_id, product_id)` (`:451-452`), so the implementation is brief-conformant, and the per-line variant warning at `:1970-1977` is a live compensating control. `pos_receipt_lines.variant_id` and `stock_movements.variant_id` both exist, so a NULL-safe variant predicate plus a two-line test is a contained fix; correcting the two overclaiming sentences and ticketing the grain is an equally defensible disposition. What is not acceptable is leaving the claim as written.
