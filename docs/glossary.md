# AutoERP Glossary — canonical domain terms (one surface per concept)

> One sentence per term. When a plan, spec, brief, migration, tile, import type or FE type uses a noun, it is
> **this** noun; the row says which table, module and operator surface are canonical and which synonyms are
> tolerated. Adding a concept = adding a row here **in the same lane** (convention
> [11-ONE-SURFACE-PER-CONCEPT](conventions/11-ONE-SURFACE-PER-CONCEPT.md)). Platform-level terms live in
> `../../../claude/glossary.md` (monorepo root `claude/glossary.md`); it points here for ERP terms (reciprocal pointer lives in the platform repo and is committed separately). Created 2026-08-29 (Session I); the Party rows are
> Session H's to keep current.

Columns: **Term** — definition · **Table / module** · **Canonical surface** (where an operator creates/edits it) ·
**Synonyms** (tolerated in UI copy only, never in code identifiers).

## Tenancy and organisation

| Term | Definition | Table / module | Canonical surface | Synonyms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tenant** | One customer account of the platform; owns one PostgreSQL database (`tenant_<uuid>`) and every row below. | `tenants` (central DB) / `Tenant` | signup (`/register`), super-admin | account, workspace |
| **Company** | A legal entity inside a tenant with its own country, currency, chart, sequences, catalogue scope and books; a tenant has ≥ 1. Catalogue keys are meant to be unique **per company** (convention 09; several are still tenant-wide today — see the tenant-only-unique ratchet baseline). | `companies` / `Company` | **Create:** the company switcher → `/company-onboarding` (`CompanySelector.tsx` → `POST /api/v1/companies`); **edit:** Settings → Companies (current company only — it cannot create). `AddCompanyModal.tsx` is an orphaned duplicate create surface rendered by nothing (owner ruling owed: delete or wire). | société, entity |
| **Location** | A physical site of a company (shop, warehouse, branch); stock, drawers and POS terminals bind to a location. Exactly one is the company default; `pos_enabled` marks a till site. | `locations` / `Location` | Settings → Locations | site, branch, warehouse, point de vente |
| **Membership** | A user's access to a company (roles are Spatie team-scoped by tenant). | `user_company_memberships` / `Identity` | Settings → Users | — |
| **General manager** | A seeded tenant role containing the revised manager grants plus company-wide lot recall and all-location Treasury authority; every active company membership held by the assignee must be unrestricted. | Existing Spatie `roles`, marked by `roles.provisioning_source` / Identity | Settings → Users (assignment) | central manager |

## Parties (Session H owns these rows)

| Term | Definition | Table / module | Canonical surface | Synonyms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Party** | Any external counterparty the company trades with; **role** (`type`) is `customer`, `supplier` or `both`; **nature** (`party_kind`, owner-ruled 2026-08-29, Session H Phase 2) is `person` (UI: *Individual / Particulier*) or `organization` (*Company / Société*). A sole trader with a matricule fiscal is an organization with `legal_form = personne_physique`; a person never carries a tax id (OQ7). The single canonical concept — a "customer" and a "supplier" are *roles* of a party, not separate entities; a private individual at the till is a person-party, never a Contact. | `partners` / `Partner` module (table name is historical) | Partners list/form; importer `ImportType::Parties` (the balance-bearing tile *"Partenaires commerciaux — … avec soldes d'ouverture"*). **Today a second tile "Partenaires" (`ImportType::Partners`) also exists and silently drops balances** — retirement + `import-tile-parties`/`customers`/`suppliers` presets are Session G lane G-9 (pending). | partner, partenaire, tiers, business partner |
| **Customer** | A party in the customer role: receivables (AR), sales documents, POS attach. | role on `partners` | same surface, preset `customers` | client |
| **Supplier** | A party in the supplier role: payables (AP), purchase documents. | role on `partners` | same surface, preset `suppliers` | vendor, fournisseur |
| **Contact** | A person **at an organization party** (name, phone, mobile, email, job title). **Never billable, never a POS customer** — a private individual who buys is a *Party* of nature Individual, not a Contact (owner ruling OQ2/R2, LEDGER D-H0-1, 2026-08-29). Two tables, both canonical and both kept: `contacts` = the person rows (the `Contact` module's model); `party_contacts` = the party↔contact link (job_title, is_primary, is_invoice_contact, is_delivery_contact). The standalone `/crm/contacts` surface is retired in Session H Phase 4; the only surface becomes the organization party's Contacts tab. | `contacts` + `party_contacts` / `Contact` module | Party detail (organization) → Contacts tab | contact person, interlocuteur, driver (Otospex fleet: the person bringing a company vehicle) |
| **Party balance** | The sum of a party's open items (invoices − credit notes − allocated payments) per company; read from the sub-ledger, never stored on the party. | documents + `payment_allocations` | Party detail → Balance | solde, encours |

## Catalogue

| Term | Definition | Table / module | Canonical surface | Synonyms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Product** | A sellable/stockable item of a company, keyed by **SKU** and optionally by a live **barcode**; both are unique per **company**. The barcode rule covers non-deleted products only, so soft deletion deliberately releases it. OR-4 keeps the separate `product_variants.barcode` key tenant-wide unique by RUL-2; the two scopes are intentional and must not be silently converged. | `products` / `Product`; `product_variants` for variants | Products list/form; importer `ImportType::Products` | article, item |
| **SKU** | The operator code of a product and the import upsert key; tenant-unique today, company-scoped after G-3a. | `products.sku` | product form | code article, référence |
| **Unit** | A unit of measure with `decimal_places` (drives every displayed quantity) inside a **unit category** that names its base unit; ≥ 19 seeded on day one (N-9). | `units`, `unit_categories` / `Uom` | Settings → Units | UoM, unité |
| **Unit text mapping** | An immutable, explicit operator decision that maps one exact non-empty source text to one company-visible unit; its company-scoped alias resolves future product-import validations only after real exact unit-code matching fails, and is never fuzzy or global. | `unit_text_mappings` / `Uom` | Settings → Units → Unmapped unit texts | explicit unit alias |
| **Category / Brand / Attribute** | Product classification rows an operator edits per company. | `categories`, `brands`, `product_attributes` | Products → settings | famille, marque |
| **Lot (batch)** | A quantity of a batch-tracked product sharing a batch number and expiry; stock of such a product is Σ lots; a `DEFAULT` lot backs openings without a number. Expiry is never invented (W4-1). | `product_batches`, `inventory_batch_stock` / `Inventory` | receipts, opening stock import, counting | batch, péremption |
| **Lot evidence** | The provenance of an allocated lot is `operator_captured`, `system_fefo_estimate` or `unknown`; FEFO suggestions/allocations must never be displayed as captured physical evidence. | Existing `pos_receipt_line_batch_allocations` (POS receipt projection), `document_lines.batch_id` (Document converters/reservations) and `stock_transfer_line_batch_allocations` (Inventory transfer orchestration) are three declared producers of the same provenance concept, with their existing writers retained; proposed annotation/captured transport is [W-LOT L5/D7](superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-parapharmacy-readiness-remediation-design.md). | Existing batch forward/backward trace consumes all applicable producers; receipt/document/transfer details and exports show the same provenance. POS guidance uses NearExpirySlot/cart; all lot surfaces require BatchExpiry and appropriate permissions. | lot provenance, batch evidence |
| **Lot identification** | A permissioned, evidence-backed split of DEFAULT/unknown holding into identified lots at the same company/product/variant/location, conserving quantity/value and retaining source lineage. | **Proposed, not shipped:** `lot_identifications`, written only by `LotIdentificationService` through the inventory reattribution contract; one flat stock justification with offsetting lot legs, no GL; unique `(tenant_id, company_id, operation_uuid)`, persisted company/location; [W-LOT L9](superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-parapharmacy-readiness-remediation-design.md). | Existing batch detail → Identify/split, gated on batches.identify + BatchExpiry/custody on API and web, with existing-tenant reseed/cache readiness before activation; counting links to the same action, never an ordinary used-lot rename. | lot identification/split, identify batch |
| **Stock level** | On-hand quantity of a product (or variant) at a location; unique per `(tenant, product, location)` for non-variant rows and `(tenant, product, variant, location)` for variant rows; the projection of stock movements. | `stock_levels` | Inventory → Stock | stock, on hand |
| **Platform enrichment trigger surfaces** | Product creation with a platform backlink and opted-in product import both write the backlink and dispatch apply work through `CatalogBacklinkDispatcher`; the read-only `products:enrich` re-apply command dispatches `ApplyCatalogEnrichmentJob` directly for products that are already linked. | `products.platform_product_id` / `Product`, `Import` | Product create form, Products import options, and the `products:enrich` maintenance command | catalogue enrichment, catalog lookup |
| **Platform image storage semantics** | A platform image is one tenant-scoped media asset keyed by `source_ref`, reused across companies in that tenant, with a distinct product attachment (including PRIMARY role) for each company's product; another tenant gets another asset. | `media_assets`, `media_attachments` / `Media`, `Product` | Product media panel (images arrive through enrichment) | shared platform image |

| Term | Definition | Table / module | Canonical surface | Synonyms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Account** | A chart-of-accounts row (code unique per company); system behaviour resolves accounts by **purpose**, never by code. | `accounts` / `Accounting` | Accounting → Chart | compte |
| **Purpose** | The functional tag of an account (`SystemAccountPurpose`: `vat_collected`, `sales_return`, `refund_write_off`, …); a fresh company must have every purpose in `ProvisioningRequiredPurposesV1::requiredPurposes()` tagged exactly once (the manifest is a partition: REQUIRED / SCOPE_REQUIRED / CONDITIONAL / SOFT — only REQUIRED is a hard day-one invariant). | `accounts.system_purpose` | seeded by country defaults; `accounting:backfill-chart-purposes` | — |
| **Tax configuration** | A VAT/stamp rate row of a country with one default per company. | `tax_configurations` / `Taxation` | Settings → Taxes | TVA, VAT rate |
| **Payment method** | A tender type (cash, card, cheque, …) of a company; exactly one is the **cash tender** (`is_cash_tender`). | `payment_methods` / `Treasury` | Settings → Payment methods | tender, mode de règlement |
| **Tender classification** | One derived economic routing class (`cash`, `maturity_instrument`, `electronic_settlement`, `non_money_destination`) distinguishes custody/collection from obligation or redemption effects. | No new stored classification column: one Treasury derivation over `payment_methods.is_cash_tender`, `has_maturity`, `instrument_kind`, existing POS `PaymentInstrumentKind` and its mandated TS mirror `apps/pos/src/lib/payment/paymentMethodKind.ts`, plus typed operation/redemption semantics. | **Tender binding** | A versioned company/branch/terminal authorization associates a tender operation with its destination or non-money handler for authoring and replay. | Existing methods/repositories remain catalogue authorities; **proposed** immutable policy revision distributed by terminal sync, transport/persistence chosen by OPEN D8 and the joint D7/D8 roadmap; W2 is the single binding orchestration, not an arbitrary device repository picker. | Existing Payment methods/repository configuration; W2-owned terminal tender-config is a narrow read projection, not a second catalogue or a bank-balance view. | **Projection lot obligation** | Durable pending/blocked/applied status for the lot arm of an accepted fiscal source, separate from aggregate receipt completion; unresolved entitlement is actionable, never disabled by inference. | **Proposed, not shipped:** `fiscal_projection_lot_obligations` child evidence, company/event/line/operation keyed; POS projection owns lot effect/status writes and W4 recovery calls the same lot-only orchestration. | **Payment** | Money received or paid, landing in a repository, later **allocated** to open items. | `payments`, `payment_allocations` | Treasury → Payments | règlement, encaissement |

## Documents and POS

| Term | Definition | Table / module | Canonical surface | Synonyms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Document** | One row of the unified `documents` table; `type` (`DocumentType`) is quote / order / invoice / credit note / delivery note / supplier variants. | `documents` / `Document` | Sales / Purchasing | — |
| **Draft** | A document before posting: `document_number` is **NULL** (deferred numbering); a numbered draft is an invariant violation. | `documents.status = draft` | document form | brouillon |
| **Document number** | The fiscal sequence value assigned at posting. Sequences are per company (`document_sequences (company_id, type, year)`), but the number itself is unique per **tenant** `(tenant_id, type, document_number)` — two companies of one tenant share the key (open hazard, baselined by the tenant-only-unique ratchet; owner ruling owed). | `document_sequences`, `documents` | assigned by posting | numéro |
| **Terminal** | A POS device identity bound to one persisted location; claims a drawer for its shift. That row is not authentication proof: W2 proposes session/token-to-terminal binding and a server-side location filter before tender-config activation. | `pos_terminals` / `POS` | POS → Terminals | caisse (device), till |
| **Shift** | The open-to-close period of a terminal on a drawer; closes with a **Z** (totals sealed, variance recorded after a **cash count**). | `pos_shifts` / `POS` | POS device; server API contract | session, clôture |
| **Session reconciliation** | A separately recorded comparison of sealed device close totals with complete fiscal-event-derived totals (independent of the Z summary, not independent of the authoring device), with explicit pending/blocked/mismatch/matched coverage; it is distinct from orphan-session closure, bank reconciliation and the separately gated Treasury repository-balance check. | **Proposed, not shipped:** `pos_session_reconciliations` / POS, written only by proposed `PosSessionReconciliationService`, reusing `ShiftExpectedCashService` for expected cash; [remediation W7](superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-parapharmacy-readiness-remediation-design.md). | Existing shift/Z detail, with append-only reconciliation runs/resolutions; no second close-authoring or automatic money-write surface. | POS close reconciliation |
| **Receipt** | A sealed POS sale (`SALE_RECEIPT` event, `unit_price` tax-inclusive); its refund is a `pos_receipt_refund`. | `pos_receipts` / `Fiscal` | POS device | ticket |
| **Import type** | One selectable importer (`ImportType`): Parties, Partners (retirement pending, lane G-9), Products, OpeningBalances, CompositeItems, ProductImages. **`StockLevels` is the retired case** (`@deprecated`, `deprecationMessage()`, excluded from `selectable()`; readable for historical `import_jobs` only). Target: every selectable type has exactly one terminal writer (RETRO G1 — today Parties and Partners share `importPartner`). | `import_jobs`, `import_rows` / `Import` | Settings → Import (tiles `import-tile-<entity>`) | — |
| **Rows to fix** | The correction export: every row of one import job that an operator must change and re-upload — invalid rows, rows whose outcome is `failed` or `opening_locked`, and rows carrying at least one warning. One writer, two containers (`failed-rows.csv` / `failed-rows.xlsx`), the mapped source columns in source order with the operator's own header spelling, plus appended `_status`, `_code`, `_message`. Round-trips through `reimport_of`, which pre-applies the original mapping. The artefact is **ephemeral** — deleted as soon as the download is streamed, and swept on job discard and at retention (spec §4.10). | `import_rows` / `Import`; `ImportRowExportService` is the single writer (`FailedRowsExportService` is deleted, not shadowed) | Import completion step and Import history row → **Download rows to fix** (`GET /api/v1/imports/{id}/failed-rows.{csv\|xlsx}`, `imports.manage`) | correction file, correction export, failed-rows CSV, error-line export, lignes à corriger |
| **Full report** | The result workbook: the **secondary**, read-only record of one import run — separate Imported / Skipped / Rejected sheets over the whole file. It is not the correction file: it is not shaped for re-upload and is never the primary action on a completion or history row. | `ResultWorkbookService` / `Import` | Import completion step and Import history row → **Full report** (secondary action beside *Download rows to fix*) | result workbook, rapport complet |
| **Partially completed** | The terminal outcome of an import that committed rows **and** either rejected rows or failed to finalize: data is in the system and work is left to do. The rule lives once, in `ImportJobOutcome` (predicate, read-model reclassification and the SQL filter expression), and is stored durably by the terminal CAS write. Jobs written before the status existed still read `completed`/`failed` in the column and are reclassified on read and in the history filter. | `import_jobs.status = partially_completed` / `ImportStatus` | Import history status pill and status filter; wizard completion step | Completed with errors, Terminé avec erreurs, partial import |
| **Re-import of** | The link from a corrected upload back to the job it corrects (`reimport_of`). Same tenant, same company and same import type are enforced (404 / 409 `IMPORT_COMPANY_MISMATCH` / 422); when the corrected file's headers still contain the original mapping's sources the mapping is pre-applied, otherwise the upload is accepted with a non-blocking `reimport_headers_changed` notice and the operator maps again. | `import_jobs.reimport_of` / `Import` | Import history row → **Re-upload corrected file** | corrected re-upload, re-import |

| **Visibility version** | **Planned (S2/S3).** Monotonic integer per company, initialised to 1 by the company-creation writer and bumped atomically on every fraud-settings controller write after that; carried by every transfer/incoming payload; ADVISORY — clients use it to discard cached transfer/incoming data on a mismatch. | `company_fraud_settings.visibility_version` / Compliance | payload field `visibility_version` / `meta.visibility_version` | — |
