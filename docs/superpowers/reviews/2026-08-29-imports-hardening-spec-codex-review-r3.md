# Session G imports hardening — adversarial spec review, round 3

**Baseline reviewed:** `a4ceeb0f5`. Source under `apps/api`, `apps/web`, `apps/pos`, and `packages/shared` has no diff from that baseline; the reviewed change is the DRAFT r3 design and its tracked supporting documents.  
**Verdict:** **REWORK**.

The r3 body materially improves M6 immutability, the opening-preview authorities, entitlement coverage, governing-cell provenance, and the migration census rationale. It still is not safe to brief to Codex. The authoritative outcome model is internally inconsistent, G-4 reintroduces chunk-delayed terminal writes, the exact-XLSX test seam cannot be implemented as written, the opening-correction diagram performs cost recomputation in the wrong order and crosses the Inventory boundary below its public service, the controller claim is not paired with a race-safe reaper clock, and the lane graph still contains multiple two-owner/dependency cycles. Independently of the pending unit-scope ruling, the live demo-unit ambiguity and the owner-mandated `units_not_seeded` invariant are both absent from the executable contract.
