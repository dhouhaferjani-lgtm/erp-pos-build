## 1. What was wrong, in one paragraph

`ArApOpeningService::validateRow()` mapped the row's `document_type` to `DocumentType::Invoice` /
`CreditNote` for **both** the AR and the AP batch — only the *partner lookup* differed (`customers()` vs
`suppliers()`). An AP opening item was therefore a **customer invoice** on the shared `HIST-INV` sequence.
`PaymentController::store()` decides supplier-ness by `type === DocumentType::SupplierInvoice`, which such a
document can never satisfy, so paying the 500.000 TND owed to the supplier took the AR arm: `Dr 512 / Cr 411`,
repository movement direction **IN**, `receivable_balance = −500.000`, `GL 401` untouched, document marked
`paid`. Separately, the AR/AP batches posted **no GL at all** while the ACCOUNTING opening posted a lump
`Dr 411 / Cr 401` with `partner_id = NULL` on every line — so no opening balance could ever reach the partner
sub-ledger and the partner pages read `0.000` against real open items.

Both are now closed, plus the double-count trap the first fix would otherwise have opened.

## 5. Design decisions a reviewer should challenge deliberately

**(a) The AR/AP open-item batch is now the single writer of the control accounts at cutover, and the GL
opening batch refuses them.** The brief specifies `Dr 119 / Cr 401` for AP and `Dr 411 / Cr 119` for AR, which
only makes sense if the open-item batch posts GL. But the campaign operator ALSO entered `411 150.000` and
`401 500.000` in the ACCOUNTING opening (§A.6) — and the shipped CSV template taught exactly that
(`401000,0.00,5000.00,Opening Payables`). Without a guard, the fix would have doubled both control accounts on
the very next tenant. So `AccountingOpeningService::validateRow()` now refuses a row on a partner control
account, at validation, before anything posts, naming the batch to use instead. This is the standard ERP rule
(control accounts are sub-ledger-only), and it makes the smoke sheet's row 0.4 satisfiable by construction.
**It is also the one behaviour change in this lane that an operator will notice.** If the reviewer or owner
prefers a warning over a refusal, that is a one-const change — but a warning leaves a wrong trial balance.

**(b) `source_type` is the document's own posting source, not `opening_balance`.** `PaymentController`
(`:549-560`) refuses to pay a supplier invoice unless a POSTED journal entry with
`source_type = 'supplier_invoice'` and `source_id = <document>` carries a Cr line on 401 tagged to the partner.
An opening supplier invoice IS a real payable and must be payable through that path, so it posts exactly that
entry. The alternative — widening the controller predicate — would have loosened a guard that exists to stop
paying unposted invoices.

**(c) The entry is created directly `Posted` with `is_historical = true`, never through
`sealAndPersistEntry()`** — the same shape `AccountingOpeningService::postBatch()` uses, because an opening
carries no fiscal hash and must stay out of the GL hash chain. The cost is that no `JournalEntryPosted` fires,
so `RefreshPartnerBalanceOnJournalEntryPosted` never runs; the batch therefore refreshes the sub-ledger
**explicitly**, once per distinct partner (`ArApOpeningService.php:383-389`). A reviewer should check that
pairing: it is the load-bearing half of W4-4 and it is not enforced by anything but the call site.

**(d) The entry carries the OPEN amount (`documents.balance_due`), not `total`.** A migrated invoice of
200.000 that the previous system already collected 50.000 against owes 150.000 at cutover. Pinned by
`ArApOpeningLedgerTest::test_the_opening_entry_carries_the_open_amount_not_the_original_total`. This agrees
with `Document::outstandingBalance()`, whose docblock already names `ArApOpeningService` as a legitimate
`balance_due` writer for this exact reason.

**(e) `PAYMENT_DIRECTION_MISMATCH` is placed BEFORE the supplier arm.** A mis-typed document is then refused
for what is actually wrong with it, rather than for the downstream symptom (`SUPPLIER_INVOICE_NOT_POSTED` — a
missing Cr-401 entry it could never have had). `PartnerType::Both` satisfies both sides. Every document type
outside AR/AP returns `true`: the guard answers one question and must not acquire opinions about advances,
expenses, POS or order-stage documents.

**(f) The new service duplicates ~20 lines of entry-number generation** rather than reaching into
`AccountingOpeningService`'s private `generateEntryNumber()`. It deliberately uses the **same** `OB-{year}-{seq}`
sequence and the **same** advisory-lock key `gl-ob-seq:{companyId}:{year}`, so the two services serialize
against each other; the docblock says so, because changing the key in one place re-opens the race. A new file
also keeps this lane out of `AccountingOpeningService::postBatch()`, which the **W4-2** lane is rewriting.

## 9. Residuals — seen, NOT touched

1. **`AgedPayablesService` still cannot see an ordinary (non-historical) supplier invoice.** The report is
   purchase-order driven: `type = PurchaseOrder, status = Posted` plus the uninvoiced accrual on
   auto-generated received POs (`AgedPayablesService.php:153-168` / `:182-240`). A real `SupplierInvoice` is
   represented by its PO, so widening my new arm to every `SupplierInvoice` would double-count. My arm is
   narrowed to `is_historical = true` for exactly that reason. **The gap is real, pre-existing and out of
   lane** — a tenant with a supplier invoice raised without a PO has an invisible payable. Worth its own row.
2. **`payment_type` never says "supplier".** `PaymentController::store():893-896` picks
   `Advance`/`DocumentPayment` from allocation-emptiness alone and ignores `$isSupplierPayment`;
   `PaymentType::SupplierPayment` has **zero writers** in `app/`, yet `CashMovementsReportService:97,:328` and
   `Payment::scopeOutgoing():313` read it, and `PaymentType::reversalSupport():186` refuses to reverse it.
   The campaign flagged the same thing ("the correct and the inverted one are indistinguishable"). Fixing it
   is a data-shape change with reporting consequences — deliberately not done here.
3. **`JournalLineData` (`Accounting/Application/DTOs/JournalLineData.php`) does not carry `partner_id`.** The
   sub-ledger dimension this lane depends on is invisible to any consumer reading a journal entry through that
   DTO (e.g. the JE detail page). The stored column is correct; the read model omits it.
4. **`PartnerBalanceService::reconcileSubledger()` has no caller and no report surface.** It computes exactly
   the control-vs-subledger difference this lane makes meaningful (`:197-213`, incl. an
   `entries_without_partner` count). Surfacing it would let an operator *prove* the cutover ties.
5. **`AccountingOpeningService::postBatch()` still writes `partner_id => null` explicitly** (`:317-325`,

   two lines become the W4-4 defect again. Left as-is; the guard is the enforcement.
6. **`OpeningBalancePosted` has no listener anywhere** — the event is dispatched and nothing consumes it.
7. **`react-doctor` findings in `apps/web/src/features/opening-balances`** — 11 warnings (array-index key,
   giant component, 3 async handlers without re-entry guards, click-without-keyboard). **All pre-existing**;

   commit hook surfaces them.
8. **`apps/web` typecheck / vitest were NOT run in this worktree** — no `node_modules` there. The FE change is

   template content (grepped).
