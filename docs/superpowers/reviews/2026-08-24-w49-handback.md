## 1. What was wrong

`GeneralLedgerService::createPOSPaymentEntry()` wrote exactly two lines — Dr repository GL account,
Cr `ProductRevenue` — both for `$payment->amount`, the **gross** tender. There was no VAT leg
anywhere on the POS arm. On the wave-4 tenant `4457 TVA collectée` had **no journal line at all**,
while `pos_receipt_vat_details` and the DGI declaration both carried the correct per-rate split.

The consequences compose badly: revenue overstated by exactly the VAT, VAT payable unrecorded, the
books and the filing disagreeing from receipt #1 — and **the trial balance still closes**, so
nothing surfaces it. Document-arm sales
(`AccountingService::createInvoiceGLEntries`) always posted the VAT leg, so the two sales channels
disagreed about the same kind of transaction.

## 2. JE shape, before and after

Worked example, **TND scale 3**, three rates on one receipt:

| rate | net | VAT | gross |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 % | 100.000 | 7.000 | 107.000 |
| 13 % | 200.000 | 26.000 | 226.000 |
| 19 % | 300.000 | 57.000 | 357.000 |
| **total** | **600.000** | **90.000** | **690.000** |

**Before** (one tender leg, 690.000 cash):

```
Dr  53   Caisse                690.000
  Cr  70x ProductRevenue       690.000      ← the whole gross, VAT included
                                            (4457: no line)
```

**After**:

```
Dr  53   Caisse                690.000
  Cr  70x ProductRevenue       600.000
  Cr  4457 POS output VAT 7.00%    7.000
  Cr  4457 POS output VAT 13.00%  26.000
  Cr  4457 POS output VAT 19.00%  57.000
```

The VAT amounts are read from the sealed `pos_receipt_vat_details` rows and never recomputed;
`600.000` is derived by SUBTRACTION (`tender − Σ VAT`), which is what makes the entry balance by
construction rather than by luck.

**Refund** (same receipt returned) is the mirror, through the same line writer:

```
  Cr  53   Caisse              690.000      ← money out
Dr  70x ProductRevenue         600.000
Dr  4457 POS output VAT reversed (refund) 7.00% / 13.00% / 19.00%   7.000 / 26.000 / 57.000
```

Sale + refund leaves cash, revenue and 4457 all flat — asserted directly in the test.

**Split tender** (690.000 = 400.000 cash + 290.000 card). One entry per leg is unchanged
(`payments.journal_entry_id` and the repository movement are still 1:1 with it), so the
receipt-level sealed VAT is apportioned by leg amount, truncated at the currency scale, with the
whole residual on the largest leg:

| rate | leg 0 (400.000) | leg 1 (290.000) | Σ = sealed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 % | 4.058 | 2.942 | **7.000** |
| 13 % | 15.073 | 10.927 | **26.000** |
| 19 % | 33.044 | 23.956 | **57.000** |
| net | 347.825 | 252.175 | **600.000** |

**Rounding and tolerance legs are untouched.** On a v3 receipt `payload.total` is the ROUNDED
amount collected and the sale value is `total − cash_rounding_adjustment`; the sealed VAT is on the
sale value. Feeding the allocator the RETAINED leg amounts leaves revenue at `net + adjustment`, and
the existing §4.6 entry moves exactly that adjustment between revenue and 6580/7580 — landing
revenue on `net` and leaving `4457` on the sealed figure. Verified end-to-end: a 690.000 sale
rounded up 0.005 posts `Dr 53 690.005 / Cr 70x 600.005 / Cr 4457 90.000` plus
`Dr 70x 0.005 / Cr 7580 0.005`, and revenue nets to 600.000 with the VAT legs untouched. **The
State is owed VAT on the sale, not on the till.**

**POS has no stamp-duty leg** — `SalesStampDutyPayable` is written only by the document arm
(`AccountingService`/`createFromCreditNote`). Nothing to leave unchanged; recorded so the next
reader does not go looking.

### Two decisions worth reviewing

**a) The resolution is LAZY, and that is load-bearing.** The first cut allocated eagerly at the top
of `apply()`. That preempted the per-leg fail-closed gates — a cross-tenant `method_code` and a
missing GL-linked repository were both reported as *VAT* problems, and three existing security tests
went red naming the wrong refusal. The split is now resolved at the moment a leg is about to post,
memoised across legs of the same receipt (`TreasuryReceiptBridge.php:473`). The security gates keep
their own errors.

**b) A receipt with no sealed rows is refused ONLY if it declares VAT.** No sealed rows plus
`pos_receipts.tax_amount = 0` is a genuinely VAT-free sale, and the whole tender IS revenue
(`PosRevenueVatSplit::vatFree()`). An empty allocation list still refuses on a VAT-bearing receipt —
`isVatFree` exists precisely so silence and zero do not look alike at the GL writer. Conversely, when
sealed rows DO exist and their sum disagrees with `tax_amount`, the projection refuses rather than
picking a winner: two authorities for one number is the W4-9 shape.

### Refusal arms (all typed, all fail-closed inside the bridge transaction)

| Reason | When |
|---|---|
| `missing_sealed_vat_details` | no sealed rows on a receipt whose `tax_amount > 0` |
| `sealed_vat_disagrees_with_receipt` | Σ sealed VAT ≠ `pos_receipts.tax_amount` |
| `vat_exceeds_tender` | sealed VAT above the retained tender (a negative revenue credit) |
| `split_does_not_reconcile` | `net + Σ VAT ≠ tender` at the GL writer's preflight |
| `non_numeric_amount` | a leg amount or sealed value that is not a plain decimal string |

Thrown from inside `DB::transaction()`, so the projection row stays pending and Horizon retries —
the projector never acknowledges a receipt it cannot book correctly.

## F-1 (CRITICAL) — the maturity-instrument POS refund reversed revenue GROSS

The reviewer is right, and the mechanism is worth stating plainly: a cheque/effet-tendered refund
never reaches `createPOSRefundReversalEntry` at all. `TreasuryReceiptBridge::handleMaturityRefundLeg()`
cancels the paper through `CancellationShape::PosRevenue` and returns, and that arm hand-wrote a
single `Dr ProductRevenue` at the **gross** instrument nominal. Symmetric while the sale also credited
gross; after r0 the sale credited `70x` net + `4457` per rate, so the refund reversed revenue by the
VAT too much and reversed no VAT at all — `4457` permanently overstated on a supported tender, while
the declaration nets that same refund out (`receipt_type='return'` → `-ABS(vat_amount)`). The lane's
own tests could not see it because their fixture seals `vat_total = 0.00`, where reversing gross and
reversing net are the same number.

**Fix.** `posRevenueAndVatLineSpecs()` is now THE decomposition, expressed as data. Three entry shapes
persist it — the sale, the cash/card reversal, and the instrument cancellation — and each persists it
differently (line ordering, an enclosing portfolio credit, a contra side), which is exactly why only
one of them may decide *what* it is. `PosRevenueVatSplit` is threaded
`cancel()` → `performCancellation()` → `createInstrumentCancellationEntry()`, **required** on the
`PosRevenue` arm (typed refusal when absent) and `assertReconciles()`d against the instrument nominal
**before** the entry row is created, so an unreversible instrument leaves no orphan Draft.

## F-2 / F-3 — the census

**F-2 (cartesian product).** `pos_receipts ⨝ vat_details ⨝ journal_entries` multiplied the rate rows
by the entry count, and the POS books **one entry per tender leg** — so every split-tender receipt
reported N× its sealed VAT. Both sides are now aggregated in **derived tables** keyed on the receipt
before they are joined. The verdict is computed in **bcmath at the receipt's own currency scale**, not
in SQL: `SUM()` over a decimal column comes back as a float on SQLite and as `numeric` on PostgreSQL,
and a deploy check must not be driver-dependent. Sums are **rounded** (`CurrencyScale::bcround`), not
truncated, so SQLite float noise cannot invent drift.

**F-3 (all-or-nothing predicate).** `COUNT(vat lines) = 0` called a receipt CLEAN when leg 0 carried a
`4457` line and leg 1 did not — precisely the mid-deploy-cutover / partially-replayed state the
command exists to find. It now compares **amounts**: per receipt, `|Σ(credit − debit)|` over
VAT-purpose lines against the sealed total. Magnitudes, because a refund legitimately DEBITS `4457`;
comparing the signed sum would flag every correct refund.

## F-4 — the transaction discount

**Decision: an explicit contra-revenue line, so the ledger's revenue base equals the declaration's
taxable base.** The device seals `subtotal + vat_total == total + transaction_discount_amount` — the
VAT is computed on the **pre-discount** base, and `EloquentVatDataRepository` reports that same
pre-discount `net_amount` as the declaration's `base_amount`. Letting the revenue credit absorb the
discount balances arithmetically but puts the ledger's implied base *below* the declared one: books
and filing agreeing about the VAT and disagreeing about what it was charged on — a quieter version of
the defect this lane exists to fix.

The treatment follows the POS's **own** ACCOUNT_CHARGE arm (`createPOSChargeEntry`, which already
books `Dr 411 total / Dr 709 discount / Cr 70x subtotal / Cr 4457 vat`), so the two POS arms now book
the same sale the same way. `SalesDiscount` (709) is already `REQUIRED` in the provisioning manifest
with `createPOSChargeEntry` as its evidence, so this adds no new provisioning dependency.

```
subtotal 600.000 · sealed VAT 90.000 · discount 50.000 · tender 640.000

Dr  53   Caisse            640.000
Dr  709  Sales discount     50.000
  Cr  70x ProductRevenue   600.000   ← == Σ sealed net_amount == the declaration's base
  Cr  4457 per sealed rate  90.000
```

The discount rides the same apportionment as the VAT, so a split-tender discounted receipt's contra
lines also add back exactly. Refunds mirror it: the contra line is a debit on the sale and a **credit**
on its reversal (a `contra` flag on the shared specs, honoured by both persisters), and a refunded
discounted sale leaves cash, revenue, `4457` and `709` all flat.

**Red proof** (production reverted): `Dr 709` is `'0.000'` where `'50.000'` is expected, on both the
sale and the refund.

**Owner question, unresolved and not decidable from the code — recorded, not assumed:** *is the
Tunisian taxable base pre- or post-transaction-discount?* This lane books what the DEVICE sealed
(pre-discount) and makes the ledger agree with the declaration that reads it. If the correct TN base
is post-discount, the fix is on the **device's** VAT computation and the declaration together, not
here — and this lane's booking would then still be internally consistent with whatever they seal.

## R2-1 (CRITICAL) — the receipt-level guard still compared against the bare tender

F-4 changed the arithmetic to `net = tender + discount − vat` and updated the **per-leg** check, but
`PosReceiptVatAllocator` still refused when `vat > tender` — i.e. whenever **`discount > net
subtotal`**, which is **every 100 %-off comp** and every comp past the net. Those receipts are
device-authorable and device-**signed**: `cartTotals.ts` clamps the discount to the GROSS subtotal, and
the payload invariant `600 + 90 == 70 + 620` holds. Nothing catches
`PosVatProjectionRefusedException`, so the projection job failed forever — no payment, no movement, no
GL entry — while the declaration still reported the sealed VAT off the same rows. The W4-9 shape from
the other side, and an availability regression versus dev, where the same receipt booked (wrongly).

The guard now compares against `tender + discount` — the gross base the device actually sealed the VAT
on — and the refusal message says `gross_base=`, not `tender=`, so it cannot mislead the next reader.

**Red proof** (allocator reverted to `349dc5a56`):

```
   PosVatProjectionRefusedException: …:vat_exceeds_tender:…:vat=90.000:gross_base=70.000
3) …::test_a_multi_leg_comp_still_assigns_the_whole_discount
   PosVatProjectionRefusedException: …:vat_exceeds_tender:…:vat=19.000:gross_base=0.000
```

Green after: the 620/70 receipt books `Dr 53 70.000 / Dr 709 620.000 / Cr 70x 600.000 / Cr 4457 90.000`

### Why the comp cases are pinned at the allocator, not end-to-end — and a finding that came out of it

The first cut asserted the 100 %-comp end-to-end through `TreasuryReceiptBridge`. It passed on SQLite
and **failed on PostgreSQL**, for a reason that has nothing to do with this lane:

```
SQLSTATE[23514]: new row for relation "pos_receipt_payments"
violates check constraint "pos_receipt_payments_amount"   -- CHECK (amount > 0)
```

A comp's only tender line is `0.000`, the payload validator requires **≥ 1** `payments[]` row
(`FiscalPayloadConstraintValidator.php:1322-1324`), and `pos_receipt_payments` has carried
`CHECK (amount > 0)` since its original migration (`2026_01_08_190640:62`). **So a 100 %-off comp
receipt cannot be projected at all on PostgreSQL — `PosCoreReceiptProjection` rejects it before the
Treasury bridge ever runs.** That is a pre-existing POS-core/schema gap upstream of W4-9 (recorded as
a residual below); the guard W4-9 owns is pinned where it lives, in `PosReceiptVatAllocatorTest`, so
the test is honest on both drivers. The general R2-1 shape (`discount > net`, positive tender) IS
pinned end-to-end, on both drivers.

## R2-2 — the census false-positived on a correctly booked instrument-tendered refund

A cheque/effet POS refund writes **no** `pos_receipt_refund` entry: the instrument lane cancels the
paper and the entry lands as `source_type='instrument'` keyed on the instrument id. The census, keyed
on `pos_receipts.id`, could not see it — so it flagged the exact receipt F-1 had just taught to reverse
`4457`, and labelled it *"never reached the GL"*, pointing the operator at re-provisioning something
booked correctly.

The census now reaches that entry the only way it can:
refund receipt → `original_receipt_id` → the original's POS `payments` (`origin='pos'`,
`fiscal_event_id`) → `payment_instruments.payment_id` → `journal_entries(source_type='instrument')`.
It is attributed to the **refund** receipt, so the sale keeps its own credit and the refund gets the
matching debit, and each reconciles against its own sealed rows.

**Red proof** (census reverted) — the reviewer's output, reproduced end-to-end on the real
sale+refund flow in `PosBridgeInstrumentRefundTest`:

```

Breakdown: 1 never reached the GL (no pos_receipt entry at all), 0 were booked with a wrong or partial VAT leg.
Failed asserting that 1 is identical to 0.
```
