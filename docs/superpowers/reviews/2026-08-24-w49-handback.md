## 1. What was wrong

`GeneralLedgerService::createPOSPaymentEntry()` wrote exactly two lines — Dr repository GL account,
Cr `ProductRevenue` — both for `$payment->amount`, the **gross** tender. There was no VAT leg
anywhere on the POS arm. On the wave-4 tenant `4457 TVA collectée` had **no journal line at all**,
while `pos_receipt_vat_details` and the DGI declaration both carried the correct per-rate split.

The consequences compose badly: revenue overstated by exactly the VAT, VAT payable unrecorded, the
books and the filing disagreeing from receipt #1 — and **the trial balance still closes**, so
nothing surfaces it. Document-arm sales
(`AccountingService::createInvoiceGLEntries`) always posted the VAT leg, so the two sales channels
disagreed about the same kind of transaction.

## 2. JE shape, before and after

Worked example, **TND scale 3**, three rates on one receipt:

| rate | net | VAT | gross |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 % | 100.000 | 7.000 | 107.000 |
| 13 % | 200.000 | 26.000 | 226.000 |
| 19 % | 300.000 | 57.000 | 357.000 |
| **total** | **600.000** | **90.000** | **690.000** |

**Before** (one tender leg, 690.000 cash):

```
Dr  53   Caisse                690.000
  Cr  70x ProductRevenue       690.000      ← the whole gross, VAT included
                                            (4457: no line)
```

**After**:

```
Dr  53   Caisse                690.000
  Cr  70x ProductRevenue       600.000
  Cr  4457 POS output VAT 7.00%    7.000
  Cr  4457 POS output VAT 13.00%  26.000
  Cr  4457 POS output VAT 19.00%  57.000
```

The VAT amounts are read from the sealed `pos_receipt_vat_details` rows and never recomputed;
`600.000` is derived by SUBTRACTION (`tender − Σ VAT`), which is what makes the entry balance by
construction rather than by luck.

**Refund** (same receipt returned) is the mirror, through the same line writer:

```
  Cr  53   Caisse              690.000      ← money out
Dr  70x ProductRevenue         600.000
Dr  4457 POS output VAT reversed (refund) 7.00% / 13.00% / 19.00%   7.000 / 26.000 / 57.000
```

Sale + refund leaves cash, revenue and 4457 all flat — asserted directly in the test.

**Split tender** (690.000 = 400.000 cash + 290.000 card). One entry per leg is unchanged
(`payments.journal_entry_id` and the repository movement are still 1:1 with it), so the
receipt-level sealed VAT is apportioned by leg amount, truncated at the currency scale, with the
whole residual on the largest leg:

| rate | leg 0 (400.000) | leg 1 (290.000) | Σ = sealed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 % | 4.058 | 2.942 | **7.000** |
| 13 % | 15.073 | 10.927 | **26.000** |
| 19 % | 33.044 | 23.956 | **57.000** |
| net | 347.825 | 252.175 | **600.000** |

**Rounding and tolerance legs are untouched.** On a v3 receipt `payload.total` is the ROUNDED
amount collected and the sale value is `total − cash_rounding_adjustment`; the sealed VAT is on the
sale value. Feeding the allocator the RETAINED leg amounts leaves revenue at `net + adjustment`, and
the existing §4.6 entry moves exactly that adjustment between revenue and 6580/7580 — landing
revenue on `net` and leaving `4457` on the sealed figure. Verified end-to-end: a 690.000 sale
rounded up 0.005 posts `Dr 53 690.005 / Cr 70x 600.005 / Cr 4457 90.000` plus
`Dr 70x 0.005 / Cr 7580 0.005`, and revenue nets to 600.000 with the VAT legs untouched. **The
State is owed VAT on the sale, not on the till.**

**POS has no stamp-duty leg** — `SalesStampDutyPayable` is written only by the document arm
(`AccountingService`/`createFromCreditNote`). Nothing to leave unchanged; recorded so the next
reader does not go looking.

### Two decisions worth reviewing

**a) The resolution is LAZY, and that is load-bearing.** The first cut allocated eagerly at the top
of `apply()`. That preempted the per-leg fail-closed gates — a cross-tenant `method_code` and a
missing GL-linked repository were both reported as *VAT* problems, and three existing security tests
went red naming the wrong refusal. The split is now resolved at the moment a leg is about to post,
memoised across legs of the same receipt (`TreasuryReceiptBridge.php:473`). The security gates keep
their own errors.

**b) A receipt with no sealed rows is refused ONLY if it declares VAT.** No sealed rows plus
`pos_receipts.tax_amount = 0` is a genuinely VAT-free sale, and the whole tender IS revenue
(`PosRevenueVatSplit::vatFree()`). An empty allocation list still refuses on a VAT-bearing receipt —
`isVatFree` exists precisely so silence and zero do not look alike at the GL writer. Conversely, when
sealed rows DO exist and their sum disagrees with `tax_amount`, the projection refuses rather than
picking a winner: two authorities for one number is the W4-9 shape.

### Refusal arms (all typed, all fail-closed inside the bridge transaction)

| Reason | When |
|---|---|
| `missing_sealed_vat_details` | no sealed rows on a receipt whose `tax_amount > 0` |
| `sealed_vat_disagrees_with_receipt` | Σ sealed VAT ≠ `pos_receipts.tax_amount` |
| `vat_exceeds_tender` | sealed VAT above the retained tender (a negative revenue credit) |
| `split_does_not_reconcile` | `net + Σ VAT ≠ tender` at the GL writer's preflight |
| `non_numeric_amount` | a leg amount or sealed value that is not a plain decimal string |

Thrown from inside `DB::transaction()`, so the projection row stays pending and Horizon retries —
the projector never acknowledges a receipt it cannot book correctly.
