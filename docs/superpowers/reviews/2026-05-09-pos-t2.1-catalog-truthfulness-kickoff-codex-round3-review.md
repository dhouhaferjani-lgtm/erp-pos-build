Same contradiction.

This is the v2 framing that R2 explicitly superseded. It was not updated in v3.

A Codex session reading this kickoff would encounter: "Step A fix contract says refactor `pullProducts` into a thin wrapper" (kickoff:170) vs. "Things you must NOT do says do NOT modify `pullProducts`" (kickoff:668). This is a load-bearing contradiction that will cause the implementation to go wrong — either the session follows the "do not modify" rule and fails to refactor (breaking the typed-error chain), or it follows the fix contract and violates the "do not modify" rule (creating a Codex review finding in its own right). MAJOR blocker.
