# Benchmark note — blind receiving: what is hidden, and is destination on-hand stock part of it? (2026-09-09)

**Question (from T-2/T-3 spec gate r9 B1, `docs/superpowers/reviews/2026-09-09-t2-t3-spec-codex-gate-r9.md`).** Rev 9 guarantees that a blind receiver cannot obtain the sent/expected/remaining quantity "from any authenticated endpoint, directly or through an equal aggregate". The gate proves this is unimplementable as written: a blind receiver keeps ordinary permissions on destination stock (stock-levels, stock matrix, POS stock/distribution, product stock, rebalance, counting reconciliation, batch/lot stock), so when another receiver posts a line the destination on-hand moves by exactly the received quantity, and `NOTHING_TO_RECEIVE` then confirms the line is exhausted. Two ways out: (a) mask every current-stock projection for blind actors while a transfer is carrying; (b) accept "current-stock delta inference" as an owner-approved residual and narrow the guarantee and the T9 oracle to document quantities.

**Domain norm (sources).**

**Reading.** In every reference the control is *documentary*: the receiver is denied the expected/shipped quantity of the document being received so the count is not anchored. No system hides the site's own on-hand inventory from the person who works there, and no system claims that a determined operator cannot infer a delta from stock reports; the residual is accepted because (1) the operator usually receives alone, (2) the reveal-and-reconcile step catches the manipulation the control is meant to deter (short-shipping collusion, waved-through shortages), and (3) hiding on-hand would break every ordinary shop task (sales, counts, replenishment).

**Recommendation for the owner (Fable does not decide):** option (b). Narrow the guarantee to "a blind actor obtains no document quantity — sent, expected, remaining, per-line or per-lot — from any transfer, receipt, replenishment, movement or notification surface, nor an equal aggregate *derived from transfer data*"; declare current-stock delta inference (on-hand, available, lot stock at the destination) an accepted residual R5 with the multi-receiver composition named; keep the §5.10 movement-feed masking (movement rows are transfer data, not stock positions); make T9 assert absence on the transfer-derived surfaces and presence-allowed on stock positions, with the positive-control table updated accordingly. Option (a) would hide a shop's own stock from its staff while any transfer is in flight, which no benchmark system does and which would break POS, counting and replenishment for the blind role.
