### MAJOR

**I-11(r2) — The ruled option-(a) widening makes "Confirm receipt" the shipped surface's ONLY terminal action on a `partially_received` transfer, and it books the whole un-arrived remainder as received-good while the page shows only the SENT quantity and the confirmation copy names no quantity. What: `StockTransferDetailPage.tsx:38` now offers Complete for `partially_received`. Clicking it calls `POST /complete` → `StockTransferService.php:310-317` → `StockTransferReceiptService.php:89-92` → `completionPayload()` `:179-196`, which emits **every line's `remainingQuantity()` as `quantity_received` with `quantity_damaged => '0.0000'`** (`:189`, `:192`), lands it at the destination (`:447-454`, `MovementType::TransferIn`), sets `Completed` (`:404-412`) and capitalises freight over the FULL sent quantity, so `freight_uncapitalized` becomes `0` (`:502-503`). The surface the operator decides from shows only the sent quantity — `StockTransferDetailPage.tsx:196`, `:225` render `line.quantity` and nothing else; there is no received/remaining column, because the hand-rolled `StockTransferLine` interface (`apps/web/src/features/stock-transfers/types/index.ts:11-24`) omits the five counters the backend DTO emits (`packages/shared/types/generated.d.ts:1276-1280` `quantity_received … quantity_remaining`; `StockTransferLineData.php:46-50`). The confirmation copy is quantity-free and, for this state, misleading: `StockTransferDetailPage.tsx:275-278` renders `detail.confirmComplete.title` = "Confirm receipt at destination?" and `detail.confirmComplete.description` = "Destination stock will be incremented and the company-wide weighted average cost will be recomputed if the transfer has additional costs." (`apps/web/src/locales/en/stock-transfers.json`), with the button labelled `detail.actions.complete` = "Confirm receipt".

Why it matters: for `in_transit` the displayed quantity IS what gets booked, so the old copy was truthful. For `partially_received` the displayed number is **not** what moves, and the correct supervisor action for a short shipment — close with a write-off (`POST /close`, `disposition = write_off`, which posts the shrinkage GL leg) — has no web surface until S4 (`TransferStatus.php:51-54` also forbids cancel). The shipped web therefore leaves an operator exactly two wrong-data outcomes on a short shipment: book the missing units as good destination stock (phantom on-hand, freight capitalised on units that never arrived, WAC diluted), or leave the transfer carrying so the remainder keeps inflating every "incoming" reader and the WAC denominator (`WeightedAverageCostService.php:119-131`). Industry baseline (Odoo/ERPNext receipts) never terminates a partial receipt by silently accepting the backorder without showing per-line received/remaining quantities.

Coverage: **no test at all covers `complete()` from `partially_received`.** Every `complete()` test starts from `in_transit` — `InventoryTransferServiceTest.php:642,645,665,714`, `StockTransferEdgeCasesTest.php:95,113`, `StockTransferCompleteConcurrencyPostgresTest.php:124,139`, `StockTransferVariantTest.php:320`. `grep -rn "partially_received\|PartiallyReceived" tests/` returns only `StockTransferReceiveTest.php:171` (second-receipt path), `TransferLegacyCompletionBackfillTest.php:143,152` (reader fixture) and two unrelated modules. Termination of a remainder is proven only by a second `POST /receive` (`StockTransferReceiveTest.php:168-180`, dest `12.0000`) and by `POST /close` (`StockTransferCloseTest.php:34-44`, dest `5.0000`, freight `70.0000`) — never by the route the new button hits.

This is a consequence of the ruled minimal scope (`plan-rev-9.md:59` lists exactly the union, three badge rows, three i18n keys and `canComplete`), not implementer drift — so the disposition is an orchestrator call, and the fix is cheap.
