- **Five §7.3 / §17.3 test-locks all present.** Lines 55-65 (Treasury active), 67-76 (lowercase strict-compared false), 78-86 (Workshop on Retail false), 88-98 (missing tenant false), 100-115 (companyId-doesn't-flip), 117-126 (token pass-through trio).
