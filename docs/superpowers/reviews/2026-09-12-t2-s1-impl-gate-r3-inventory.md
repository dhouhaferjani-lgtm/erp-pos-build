Mitigating facts I verified: the members are **mandated by spec rev 11:190** and reproduced in plan rev 10 `:2547-2548`, `:2597-2598`, so this is authority-following, not drift; and there is **no consumer** — `grep -rn 'totalReceived\|totalDamaged\|totalWrittenOff' apps/api/app` returns only the two event classes and the writer, and `TransferReceiptEventStreamTest` asserts none of the four, so nothing decides on the value today. *Minimum fix, ascending cost:* (a) orchestrator ruling recorded in §7.12 **plus** a one-line docblock on both event classes stating the totals are informational, undefined for mixed-unit transfers, and that no consumer may compute from them (the consumer census above is the evidence) — ~6 lines, no behaviour change; or (b) drop the four members and the `$totals` accumulator before V1 seals (also ~15 lines, since nothing reads them), keeping `lineCount`, which is already on both events.
