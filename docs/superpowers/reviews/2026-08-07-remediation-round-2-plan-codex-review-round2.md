## New Findings

### BLOCKER — NEW in v2: Phase R names questions but is not an executable decision gate

- **Plan target:** Phase R and its consumers (`plan.md:57-76,115-143`).
- **Concrete failure scenario:** the orchestrator can reach Wave 2 without a defined implementable contract: R-a does not map its two tax-base answers to writer/backfill behavior; R-b has three nested accounting choices and does not actually block G; R-c lacks alternatives/criteria for several decisions; and R-d leaves A2/A3’s secondary rulings unowned. Implementers must invent fiscal/accounting behavior or stop mid-lane. R-e and R-f can be “answered” without any scheduled deliverable. The detailed per-ruling evidence is in the audit below.

### MAJOR — NEW in v2: R2-K closes only the frozen-repository vector and dispatches before its own ruling

- **Plan target:** “Wave 1 (no shared surfaces, dispatch immediately)” and R2-K (`plan.md:80,100-102`).
- **Concrete failure scenario:** K can be marked complete after a frozen-repository preflight/recoverability fix while a membership revoked mid-request still seals a `DEPOSIT_RECEIPT` that cannot project. The source ticket explicitly identifies this second vector and calls for a membership preflight plus an authz reachability check (`2026-08-05-deposit-residual-seal-before-resolve-vectors.md:65-80`). The lane has no tenancy-authz gate, and its “recoverability ruling” is neither in Phase R nor decided before immediate dispatch.

### MAJOR — NEW in v2: F5’s consumer map is factually wrong and drops the credit-note-blind allocation services

- **Plan target:** F5, “SmartPayment + 4 PaymentController writers” (`plan.md:135`).
- **Concrete failure scenario:** a fully credit-noted invoice can remain eligible for automatic or manual smart allocation after F5 is declared done. The source ticket has one `PaymentController::storeMultiple()` path, two `PaymentAllocationService` methods, and `SmartPaymentController::previewAllocation()` (`2026-08-06-l2-remaining-balance-due-consumers.md:22-50`); it describes them as read/cap paths, not “4 PaymentController writers” (`:62-87`). A literal dispatch from v2 can omit both `PaymentAllocationService` fixes.

### MAJOR — NEW in v2: R-b is attached to the wrong lane and is missing from the merge graph

- **Plan target:** R-b, R2-F, and R2-G (`plan.md:61-62,130-142`).
- **Concrete failure scenario:** if the expert requires the timbre residue to be split, the work changes `AccountingService::residualPlan()`, the Tunisian chart seeder, and a tenant backfill/restatement posture (`2026-08-05-tn-timbre-account-carries-rounding-noise.md:53-76`). R2-G is a Q2 expense-VAT/backfill/FE lane and has no GL/accounting gate; it is blocked only on R-a. R2-F also changes `AccountingService` (`2026-08-06-l2-cogs-and-ap-unreversed-on-cancel.md:10-19`) but has no order against the R-b outcome. The result is either silent omission of N-9 or an unordered AccountingService/migration expansion inside the wrong lane.

### MAJOR — NEW in v2: the completeness assertion is false; five appendix obligations remain silently unassigned

- **Plan target:** “Every appendix ticket is now either assigned ... or EXPLICITLY deferred” and the complete deferral block (`plan.md:3-7,149-156`).
- **Concrete failure scenario:** the round can close while reachable or compounding defects remain outside every lane and deferral record:
  - `2026-08-06-l2-gl-gate-minor-followups.md` is absent; supplier invoices remain invisible in aged payables (`:79-98`) and the reversal audit/ordering/coupling follow-ups have no disposition (`:13-65,100-129`).
  - `2026-08-06-l6-partners-followups.md` is absent; a partner referenced by financial/operational tables can still soft-delete into invisibility (`:14-38`).
  - Phase 0 lists disposition of the existing C-2 warehouse receipt (`plan.md:48-50`) but no lane fixes the fixture that compounds a new warehouse fiscal receipt on every run (`2026-08-06-c2-fixture-terminal-location.md:51-69`).
  - `2026-08-06-pos-product-images-never-populated.md` is neither assigned nor explicitly deferred; it is a distinct coordinated POS/server release defect, not the deferred “device Z gross-as-net” program (`:1-6,56-80`; `plan.md:155-156`).
  - `2026-08-07-repobal-lane-followups.md`’s admin-gated `allow_negative` UI control is absent, leaving the owner-ruled field API-only (`:6-11`). R2-I covers only that ticket’s rollback-test trap.

### MAJOR — NEW in v2: R2-L’s gate does not cover the risks bundled into the new lane

- **Plan target:** R2-L and its `precision + FE` gate (`plan.md:103-108`).
- **Concrete failure scenario:** the lane can change the `credit-notes.confirm` permission across seeder, route, and FE without tenancy-authz review, while its backend draft/confirm tax-pipeline change receives no Document/Taxation review. The source ticket explicitly requires deciding and aligning the permission across all three surfaces and describes the rounding unification as certification-relevant (`2026-08-03-f2f3-regate-carryovers.md:6-14,31-35`). This is exactly the reviewer-risk mismatch the new matrix was meant to prevent.

### MAJOR — NEW in v2: D’s stated merge order is impossible

- **Plan target:** the cross-lane rule and R2-D (`plan.md:19-20,145-147`).
- **Concrete failure scenario:** a rule cannot “land LAST” and also already be present for every PHP lane to “rebase over it.” If business lanes merge first, the repo-wide rule can turn the integrated tree red only after their specialist gates and merges are over; if the rule lands first, D is not last. The plan names no final full-PHP repair/re-gate after D, so its merge graph has no executable ordering for the static-analysis ratchet.

### MAJOR — NEW in v2: the cross-tenant deferral’s stated reason does not cover its operational risk, and its T-10 does not exist

- **Plan target:** deferred `2026-08-05-cross-tenant-annotation-ast-check` (`plan.md:152-154`) and the complete Phase-2 list (`plan.md:158-175`).
- **Concrete failure scenario:** the plan calls the whole ticket “test/ops launch-debt” and assigns it to a “T-10 backlog line,” but Phase 2 defines only T-1 through T-9. The same ticket proves `fiscal:backfill` is absent from the production CLI while an owner checklist refers to it and requires a register-or-delete decision (`2026-08-05-cross-tenant-annotation-ast-check.md:106-133`). An operator can believe a fiscal backfill ran when no command exists, and no owner, ruling artifact, or downstream task in v2 closes that branch. This is not justified by the stated test-debt deferral.
