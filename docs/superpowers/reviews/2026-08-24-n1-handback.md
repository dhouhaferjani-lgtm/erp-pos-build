Taken the gate's **preferred** fix, not the minimal one — the draft path had no tax resolution at all, and
patching the frontend to keep sending a rate would have left the endpoint unable to read a field its only

## Finding 3 [IMPORTANT] — the 7 stale campaign-tenant lines. EXTENDED, not documented away.

The gate is right that the handback's "credit note" framing was wrong: none of those rows is posted or
sealed, all are repairable, and `CopiesDocumentData::copyLine()` (`:141`) copies `tax_rate` verbatim, so
converting one after this deploy would mint a **new** wrong-VAT invoice — worse than the historical rows.
Extended the migration rather than writing a checklist step, per the recommendation:

`…_backfill_products_tax_rate_from_tax_configuration_n1.php` `repairUnpostedDocumentLines()` (+ `retotal()`):

- Repairs `document_lines.tax_rate` from the product's **configuration** (not from the just-repaired
  `products.tax_rate`, so the two halves stay independent), for lines whose document passes **three
  independent guards, all required**: `status IN ('draft','confirmed')`, `fiscal_status = 'DRAFT'`,
  `fiscal_hash IS NULL`. Nothing posted, sealed or voided is reachable; a posted document has GL entries
  derived from its tax and a sealed one is hash-chained — those are credit-note territory, and both are
  pinned by their own test.
- Lines with no product (free-text/service) state their own rate and are never touched.
- **Re-totals the affected documents** through `DocumentTotalsCalculator::recalculate()` — the module's own
  calculator, the same one the draft path calls — so TN stamp duty, line discounts and partner exemptions
  are handled by the code that owns them instead of re-derived in SQL. Scale comes from the document's own
  currency (same guard as `CopiesDocumentData::recalculateTotals()`), so it is safe in the
  no-`CompanyContext` world of `tenants:migrate` (rule 20). Without this the header would keep the
  19 %-derived total while the lines said 7 % — a document contradicting itself on paper.
- Its **own savepoint** and its own counters: the gate line is now
  `status=ok repaired=N doc_lines=M docs_retotalled=K`. A failure in this half cannot roll back the product
  half, which is the part that stops new drift.
- Idempotent: the predicate is "the two disagree", and `recalculate()` writes through Eloquent, which issues
  no UPDATE when the recomputed totals equal the stored ones.
