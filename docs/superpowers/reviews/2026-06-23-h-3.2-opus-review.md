# H-3.2 Supplier Invoice GL Posting — Opus Adversarial Review

Date: 2026-06-23
Item: H-3.2 (work-list `2026-06-22-balance-conventions-audit/work-list.md`)
Commit: 53115efaa6d3576f9c13df0522be9e712c7b8184 ("Phase 0.1.14: Post supplier invoice GL entries")
Reviewer: Opus (adversarial, refutation-first). Prior review was Codex-only + a self-authored "Opus fallback" that is NOT an independent Opus pass.

## Summary

The patch wires `PurchaseOrderConfirmed` → `PurchaseOrderConfirmedListener` →
`GeneralLedgerService::createSupplierInvoiceJournalEntry()`, posting a partner-tagged
`supplier_invoice` GL entry and refreshing `payable_balance` after commit, with a
read-then-write idempotency guard. The transaction/afterCommit plumbing, the
partner-tag/sign convention, and the source linkage are correct and well-tested at
the helper level. However the slice has two accounting-model problems that the tests
do not exercise and the Codex/fallback reviews did not catch:

1. AP + full **expense** is recognized at **purchase-order confirmation** (a supplier
   commitment, before goods receipt / before a supplier invoice exists). This both
   recognizes the cost in the wrong period and collides with the perpetual
   Inventory→COGS engine, which credits an Inventory asset that purchases never debit.
2. The listener books the document's **entire `tax_amount` to VAT Deductible
   (recoverable)**, even though `tax_amount` includes **non-recoverable taxes and
   stamp duty** (Tunisia). That misstates recoverable input VAT.

Because at this commit `postEntry()` does not yet reject unbalanced entries
(balance guard arrives later in c54a9d6da "Phase 0.1.15"), a stale `subtotal` (which
`confirm()` never re-saves) can post a silently unbalanced AP entry.

Verdict: **NEEDS-REVISION**. The plumbing is good; the accounting semantics and the
tax-recoverability mapping are wrong for any non-trivial (taxed / stockable / stamp-duty)
purchase order, and the production confirmation test only covers the zero-tax,
subtotal==total happy path.

## BLOCKER

None that is unconditionally data-corrupting on the tested happy path. The most severe
issues are conditional (taxed / stamp-duty / stale-subtotal POs) and are filed as HIGH.
If the deployment targets Tunisia (stamp duty is in scope per
`2025_12_30_107000_add_stamp_duty_to_documents.php`) treat H1+H2 as launch-blocking.

## HIGH

### H1 — AP + expense recognized at PO confirmation is the wrong recognition event and conflicts with the perpetual inventory/COGS model
`PurchaseOrderConfirmedListener::handle()` posts a posted `supplier_invoice` entry that
debits **PurchaseExpenses** and credits **SupplierPayable** at PO *confirmation*:

`apps/api/app/Modules/Accounting/Listeners/PurchaseOrderConfirmedListener.php:43-58`
```php
$purchaseExpenseAccount = Account::findByPurposeOrFail($event->companyId, SystemAccountPurpose::PurchaseExpenses);
$this->glService->createSupplierInvoiceJournalEntry(
    ... totalAmount: $purchaseOrder->total ?? '0.00',
    netAmount: $purchaseOrder->subtotal ?? '0.00',
    expenseAccountId: $purchaseExpenseAccount->id, ...);
```

A confirmed PO is a *commitment to buy*, not a received good and not a received supplier
invoice. The event's own docblock states "Confirmed purchase orders are ready for goods
receipt" — i.e. goods are NOT yet received. Recognizing both a payable and a P&L expense
at this point overstates AP and expense before any liability has crystallized.

Worse, the codebase runs a **perpetual** inventory model: `createCOGSEntry()` debits
CostOfGoodsSold and credits the **Inventory** asset on sale
(`GeneralLedgerService::createCOGSEntry()`, accounts `CostOfGoodsSold` + `Inventory`).
For that to balance over the lifecycle, purchases of stockable goods must be capitalized
to **Inventory** at goods receipt (Dr Inventory / Cr AP), not expensed at PO confirm.
`GoodsReceiptService` posts **no** GL entry (grep: no `glService`/`JournalEntry`/
`GeneralLedger` usage), so Inventory is never debited from purchasing. Net effect under
this slice: purchases hit PurchaseExpenses at PO confirm, while COGS later credits an
Inventory asset that was never debited → Inventory drifts negative and cost is
double-counted (once as PO-confirm expense, again as COGS). This is an internally
inconsistent GL model, not just a timing nit.

Recommendation: recognize AP at goods receipt or supplier-invoice receipt, capitalize
stockable purchases to Inventory (Dr Inventory/Cr GRNI or AP), and reserve PurchaseExpenses
for non-stock/service purchases. At minimum, gate the listener so it does not expense
stockable lines that flow through the COGS engine.

### H2 — Entire `tax_amount` is booked to VAT Deductible, including non-recoverable tax and stamp duty
The listener passes the document's full `tax_amount` as the VAT-deductible leg:

`apps/api/app/Modules/Accounting/Listeners/PurchaseOrderConfirmedListener.php:50`
```php
vatAmount: $purchaseOrder->tax_amount ?? '0.00',
```
and the GL helper debits all of it to the `VatDeductible` account
(`GeneralLedgerService::createSupplierInvoiceJournalEntry()`, "VAT Deductible (if applicable)").

But `tax_amount` = `TaxCalculationResult::totalTax` = sum of **all** calculated taxes,
recoverable or not (`TaxCalculationService` line ~185: `$totalTax = bcadd($lineItemsTaxTotal, $documentTaxTotal, $scale)` — recoverability is tracked per `CalculatedTax::isRecoverable` but never subtracted from the total). Stamp duty is "NEVER recoverable"
(`TaxCalculationService::determineRecoverability()` step 1) and non-VAT-registered
companies recover nothing. The non-recoverable portion is allocated into line cost by
`LandedCostService::allocateCostsAndTaxes()` (`non_recoverable_tax`, `non_recoverable_tax_total`),
yet this listener still debits the whole `tax_amount` to recoverable input VAT.

Consequence: for any PO with stamp duty (Tunisia) or non-recoverable VAT, the company's
VAT-deductible (input VAT recoverable from the tax authority) is overstated and the
non-recoverable tax is misclassified — a fiscal misstatement. The patch should debit
only the **recoverable** tax to VatDeductible and capitalize the non-recoverable portion
to the expense/inventory leg (which is exactly what `non_recoverable_tax` already tracks).

### H3 — Stale `subtotal` can post a silently unbalanced entry (no balance guard at this commit)
`PurchaseOrderService::confirm()` recomputes and re-saves `tax_amount` and `total` from
`$taxResult` but **never re-saves `subtotal`** (`confirmAndAllocateCosts()` updates only
`tax_amount` and `total`). The listener then reads the **stored draft `subtotal`** as the
expense (net) leg and the **recomputed `total`** as the AP leg. Balance therefore requires
`stored_subtotal + stored_tax_amount == total`, i.e. that the caller's draft `subtotal`
exactly equals the tax engine's line-derived subtotal (`calculateSubtotal()` = Σ line_total
− discount). Any draft where the persisted `subtotal` diverges from the recomputed
line-based subtotal (rounding, mismatched draft header, discount edits) yields
`debit ≠ credit`.

At commit 53115efaa, `postEntry()` performs **no** debit==credit validation (the reject-unbalanced
guard lands later in `c54a9d6da` "Phase 0.1.15: Reject unbalanced journal posting"). So an
unbalanced supplier-invoice entry posts silently and corrupts the trial balance and
payable subledger. Fix: source the net leg from `$taxResult->subtotal` (or re-save subtotal
on confirm) rather than the stale stored value, and/or derive the expense leg as
`total − recoverableVat` so the entry balances by construction.

### H4 — Actorless confirmation silently skips AP recognition
The listener resolves the actor via `User::query()->where('tenant_id', ...)->find($event->confirmedBy)`
and returns early if null (`PurchaseOrderConfirmedListener::handle()` `if ($user === null) return;`).
`confirmed_by` is nullable (`2025_12_24_133828_add_confirmation_tracking_to_documents.php`:
`onDelete('set null')`) and `confirmAndAllocateCosts()` sets it from `auth()->id()`, which is
null in queued/console/import contexts. A PO confirmed without a bound auth user therefore
produces **no AP entry and no payable balance** — silently. For an AP recognition path this
is a correctness gap: it should fall back to a system actor (the H-2 series established the
actor/`currencyCode` fallback pattern) or fail loudly, not no-op.

## MEDIUM

### M1 — Idempotency guard is read-then-write with no unique constraint or row lock
`alreadyPosted()` does `JournalEntry::where(source_type, source_id)->exists()` then inserts.
`(source_type, source_id)` is only a **non-unique index**
(`2025_11_30_100000_create_journal_entries_table.php:44 $table->index(['source_type','source_id'])`),
not a unique constraint. Two concurrent deliveries of the same event would both pass the
`exists()` check and double-insert. The `test_purchase_order_confirmed_listener_is_idempotent`
test only covers **serial** re-delivery; it asserts nothing about concurrency. The Codex
review's claim that "a repeated event delivery is covered by a test and does not create a
duplicate" is true only for the serial case. For a true idempotency guarantee add a unique
partial index on `(company_id, source_type, source_id)` for this source type or a row lock.

### M2 — Production confirmation test only covers the zero-tax / subtotal==total happy path; "VAT path covered" is misleading
`test_confirmation_posts_supplier_invoice_gl_and_refreshes_payable_balance` uses
`subtotal=1000, tax_rate=0`, so it never exercises the VAT-deductible leg, the
recoverable-vs-non-recoverable split (H2), or the stale-subtotal imbalance (H3). The
Codex review defers VAT coverage to `GLIntegrationTest::test_supplier_invoice_creates_correct_journal_entry`,
but that test calls the **helper directly with hand-fed, pre-balanced numbers**
(`total 119 = net 100 + vat 19`) — it does not exercise the **listener's** mapping of
document `subtotal`/`tax_amount`/`total`, which is exactly where H2/H3 live. The tests
would pass even if the listener mapping were wrong for any taxed PO → false confidence.

### M3 — `total`/`subtotal`/`tax_amount` read from a re-fetched Document rather than the event payload
The event carries `total` and `currency` but the listener re-fetches the Document and reads
`->total`, `->subtotal`, `->tax_amount`. This is the source of H3 (event total vs stored
subtotal mismatch). Domain events are meant to carry the data needed by listeners;
re-fetching couples the AP entry to mutable document state and to whatever else may have
updated the row between dispatch and handling. Prefer passing the authoritative
net/recoverable-vat/total triple on the event (or `$taxResult`) so the entry is
self-consistent.

## LOW

### L1 — Document amounts are `decimal(15,2)` but cast `decimal:3` and posted at scale 3/4
`documents.subtotal/tax_amount/total` are stored at 2dp
(`2025_11_30_080000_create_documents_table.php`) while the model casts `decimal:3` and GL
posts at scale 3 / balances at 4. No float is introduced (casts emit strings), so this is
not a precision-contract violation, but the 2dp storage vs 3dp posting boundary is an
existing inconsistency this slice now relies on. Not introduced here; noted for awareness.

### L2 — Process docs overstate completion
The coordination log marks "H-3.2 DONE / H-3 DONE" and the work-list flips H-3 to DONE,
but the acceptance criterion "Supplier invoice and supplier payment production actions
create posted, partner-tagged SupplierPayable lines" is satisfied only for untaxed POs;
taxed/stockable/stamp-duty POs are mis-booked (H1/H2). The "true Opus review remains
pending" disclaimer is accurate — this review is that pass and it does not concur with DONE.

## Verdict

**NEEDS-REVISION.** Transaction safety, partner tagging, sign convention, and source
linkage are correct, but recognizing expense+AP at PO confirmation (H1), booking all tax
incl. non-recoverable/stamp duty to VAT Deductible (H2), and the stale-subtotal imbalance
risk with no balance guard at this commit (H3) make the GL output wrong for any taxed or
stockable purchase order. The "DONE" status and the prior PASS reviews do not hold up.
