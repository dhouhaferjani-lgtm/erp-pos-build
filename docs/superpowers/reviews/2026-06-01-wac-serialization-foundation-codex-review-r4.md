## Caller Surface Sweep

I challenge the sweep disposition for `ReturnNoteService`. It is true that the only explicit `lockForUpdate()` before the seam call is a fiscal-chain `Document` lock at `apps/api/app/Modules/Document/Domain/Services/ReturnNoteService.php:87-93`, not a `Product` or `StockLevel` lock. That is not sufficient: `confirm()` opens one transaction at `apps/api/app/Modules/Document/Domain/Services/ReturnNoteService.php:71-78`, `receiveStockBack()` loops return-note lines at `apps/api/app/Modules/Document/Domain/Services/ReturnNoteService.php:147-149`, and each physical line calls `recordReturn()` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Document/Domain/Services/ReturnNoteService.php:183-191` without any up-front sorted multi-product advisory acquire. `recordReturn()` itself acquires only the current single product at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:376-377`, so a return note with product order `[B,A]` can hold `B` and then wait on `A` while a sorted multi-product caller has already taken `A` and waits on `B`.

`ApplyStockAdjustmentsOnCountingCompleted` matches the sweep disposition. The listener iterates items without wrapping the whole loop in a transaction at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Listeners/ApplyStockAdjustmentsOnCountingCompleted.php:46-84`; its pre-call stock read is an unlocked `value('quantity')` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Listeners/ApplyStockAdjustmentsOnCountingCompleted.php:67-69`; each call to `StockAdjustmentService::adjust()` is therefore its own seam transaction at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Listeners/ApplyStockAdjustmentsOnCountingCompleted.php:74-81` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Domain/Services/StockAdjustmentService.php:445-494`.

`ImportService` matches the sweep disposition for the batch execution path. `executeImport()` processes valid rows one at a time at `apps/api/app/Modules/Import/Services/ImportService.php:264-284`, wraps each row in its own transaction at `apps/api/app/Modules/Import/Services/ImportService.php:266-273`, and the stock-level row path calls `InventoryService::upsertStockLevel()` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Import/Services/ImportService.php:389-395`. `upsertStockLevel()` is self-locking with a single-product `ProductCostLock` call at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/InventoryService.php:39-62`.

The sweep also missed `InventoryOpeningService::postBatch()`. It is listed by the architecture test as a ProductCostLock seam at `apps/api/tests/Architecture/InventoryCostLockCoverageTest.php:31-42`, opens one transaction for the whole posting at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/InventoryOpeningService.php:224-350`, loops valid rows at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/InventoryOpeningService.php:230-301`, and acquires only one product per row at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/InventoryOpeningService.php:247-297`. Because the row order is `row_number` from `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/InventoryOpeningService.php:212-216`, not sorted unique product id order, two opening batches containing overlapping products in opposite row order can AB-BA on advisory locks.

For `GoodsReceiptService`, I found no new defect in the extracted closure itself: the up-front advisory call surrounds `processReceiptLines()` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:78-85`, the loop starts only inside that method at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:100-108`, and `recordPurchase()` is reached at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:157-165`. Over-acquiring advisory locks for non-received or non-physical product ids is not a correctness problem: ids are collected before filtering at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:69-76`, while the processing loop skips null product ids at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:128-130` and non-physical products at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:141-146`.

I did find one tenant/company hardening issue in `GoodsReceiptService`: an explicit PO location is taken from the unscoped `Document::location` relation at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:51-53`, and that relation is a plain `belongsTo(Location::class)` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Document/Domain/Document.php:202-204`. The product lookup is correctly scoped to the PO tenant/company at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:141-144`, but `recordPurchase()` creates a missing stock level with `company_id` from the captured `Location` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:112-119` after querying under the product's company at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:105-110`. The controller normally resolves PO locations before creation at `apps/api/app/Modules/Document/Presentation/Controllers/PurchaseOrderController.php:203-219`, but this receipt seam should still assert `$location->company_id === $purchaseOrder->company_id` or reload the location scoped by the PO company.

3. Severity: MINOR | `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/GoodsReceiptService.php:51` | `GoodsReceiptService` uses the PO's unscoped `location` relation as the WAC location source. Concrete fix: reload the receipt location with `Location::query()->where('company_id', $purchaseOrder->company_id)->findOrFail($location->id)` or throw if the captured location's `company_id` differs from the PO company before entering `processReceiptLines()`.

## Summary

The round-3 fixes in `2fda2b6c5` resolved the two specifically flagged issues in `StockTransferService::moveSourceToInTransit()` and `GoodsReceiptService::receiveGoods()`: the transfer cost snapshot now occurs after the source stock row lock, the product loads are unlocked, and goods receipt now takes a sorted up-front advisory lock. However, the final caller sweep does not converge: `ReturnNoteService` and `InventoryOpeningService::postBatch()` still have multi-product, single-transaction advisory acquisition in caller/import order. I cannot confirm the claimed absence of residual deadlock cycles until those two paths acquire all affected product advisory locks sorted and up front; the branch remains `REQUEST-CHANGES` before merge.
