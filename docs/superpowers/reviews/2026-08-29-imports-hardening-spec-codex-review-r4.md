# Session G imports hardening — adversarial spec review, Round 4

**Reviewed:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-29-imports-hardening-design.md`, DRAFT r4.1  
**Prior register:** G-R46..G-R56  
**Gate verdict:** **REWORK**

The r4.1 revision closes the mechanical `.xls`, correction-order, sign-authority, retention, and identifier-continuity questions. It is not yet an executable contract for all remaining lanes. The blocking defects are in work still to be built: invalid rows have no authoritative terminal outcome; G-6a cannot atomically publish counters or restore a failed zero-row claim from the stated APIs; and G-4 has neither a legal cross-module unit-catalog seam nor a vocabulary that can satisfy the promised “accepted list == surfaced list” invariant. These are contract defects, not requests to revisit the pre-approved design.
