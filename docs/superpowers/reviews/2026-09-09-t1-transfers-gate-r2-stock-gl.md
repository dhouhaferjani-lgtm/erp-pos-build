## Seams handed to T-2 (facts with lines, for the receipt / discrepancy lane)

1. **Transfers post ZERO GL today — including freight.** No GL collaborator in the transfer call graph (`StockTransferService.php:72-77`, `StockAdjustmentService.php:82-90`, `WeightedAverageCostService.php:49-53`), no listener on the three transfer events, no GL subscriber on `StockMovementRecorded`. Any T-2 receipt/write-off/shrinkage decision is the **first** GL posting on this document type: it must go through `InventoryGlPostingBuffer::flushIfOutermost()` (enforced by `app/PHPStan/Rules/InventoryGlPostingViaBufferOnly.php:35-43`), never a direct `journal_entries` insert.
2. **Freight capitalization is document-less and GL-less.** `transfer_cost` is a free-typed money field capitalized into company-wide WAC at completion (`StockTransferService.php:396-402`, `:631-709`), residual absorbed by the last cost-bearing line at 4 dp (`:687-691`). No carrier document, no expense/clearing credit. A partial-receipt or write-off model in T-2 must decide what happens to already-capitalized freight when the goods never arrive.
3. **Capitalization ordering is load-bearing.** The transfer must be marked `Completed` before `capitalizeTransferCost`, otherwise the units are counted twice in the WAC denominator (`StockTransferService.php:386-394` + `WeightedAverageCostService.php:119-125`). A `partially_received` intermediate status that is neither `InTransit` nor `Completed` silently changes that denominator — decide it explicitly.
4. **In-transit units live in no stock_level row and no batch row.** They exist only in `stock_transfer_lines` (+ `stock_transfer_line_batch_allocations`). WAC counts them as owned (`WeightedAverageCostService.php:119-127`); the lot ledger does not. Per-location `SUM(inventory_batch_stock) == stock_levels.quantity` therefore holds at both endpoints *because* transit is outside both — any T-2 "transit location" would have to write both ledgers at that location or the invariant flips.
5. **Movement rows are rewritten after creation.** `markMovementAsTransfer` (`StockTransferService.php:745-752`) `update()`s the row created by `receive()`/`issue()`, and the announcing event still carries the hardcoded label `movementType: 'receipt'` / `'issue'` (`StockAdjustmentService.php:194,213`, `:338,:344`) while `reference_type/reference_id` (read from the same mutated model in the `afterCommit` closure) point at the `StockTransfer`. Consumers that classify by event label see a receipt, consumers that read the row see `transfer_in`. T-2 receipt events must not inherit this split.
6. **Settlement is demand-only and quantity-blind.** `SettleRequestsOnTransferInitiated.php:46-71` fulfils *every* open request at (company, destination, product, variant) grain regardless of shipped quantity; live-pinned at `ReplenishmentEdgeCasesTest.php:91-105` (a 2-unit transfer fulfils a 5-unit request). It writes no stock and no GL; failures are swallowed and logged (`:33-40`).
7. **Cancel from `in_transit` restocks the source with `TransferIn` movements** (`StockTransferService.php:465,:478`) and the reopen path merges demand into the surviving open grain under a PG partial unique (`ReplenishmentEdgeCasesTest.php:126-154`). A T-2 `close`/partial-receipt disposition must reuse that restock loop or explicitly justify a write-off — and, if it writes off, book it (movement + GL), not just flag rows.
