## 1. Problem, definitions, scope

**Problem.** A transfer is decremented at source on `store` and incremented at destination by a quantity-less `complete` (`apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/StockTransferService.php:346-383`): the receiver cannot say "11 of 12 arrived, one broken". No receipt document exists, so nothing can be blind; the three in-transit readers derive incoming from `status = in_transit` alone (`apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/LocationStockQueryService.php:137-164,217-245`, `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/StockMatrixQueryService.php:390-408`, `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:117-127`) and cannot express a partial receipt. Nobody is told a transfer is coming or arrived short.

**Definitions (M2 r1 — two different things).**
- **Open remainder**: per line, `sent − received − damaged − written_off − returned > 0` while the transfer is `in_transit` or `partially_received`. No reason, no alert, `has_discrepancy` false.
- **Confirmed discrepancy**: (a) **damaged** units declared on a receipt — reason required, alert at receipt; (b) **short** units confirmed by a **close** — write-off or return, reason required, alert at close. Shortness is never asked of a receiver (D2). A shortage confirmed at close is a **transfer-level fact**: its event actor is the closer, and it is never attributed to any receiver as that receiver's own report (§4.4).

**In scope.** T-2: receipt document (per-line received/damaged, lot grain); remainder stays in transit; close with `disposition: write_off | return_to_source` (OQ-1); quantity-based in-transit derivation; stored header + per-line events with a real aggregate anchor; `complete` as the zero-discrepancy delegate for non-blind actors only. T-3: `blind_receiving`; receiver view by construction; blind closure over every reachable surface incl. the POS clients (§5.0, §8b); reconciliation; mobile contract. T-4-min: two DB notifications (§7).

**Out of scope** (ticket each): push/Expo; `ReplenishmentRequestedNotification`; mobile screens (M-1); T-3b implementation; inter-company transfers (`StockTransferService.php:96-101` still refuses); over-receipt tolerance; alert threshold (v1 = any); **receipt `void`/reversal** — an over-reported receipt has no reversal in v1: a supervisor corrects on-hand with a stock-adjustment document (which refuses destructive reasons on every batch-tracked product, `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/StockAdjustmentDocumentService.php:520-531` — such products go through the lot write-off path), while receipt rows, counters, reconciliation and terminal state stay as posted; **receiver note** (D2 ticket T-2b); **analytics read model / dashboard** (T-4 or later — this lane guarantees the stored facts and ships the reference query, §4.4); shrinkage account provisioning (exists: `apps/api/app/Modules/Accounting/Application/Services/InventoryVarianceAccountProvisioner.php:162-169`, purpose `InventoryShrinkageExpense`, `apps/api/app/Modules/Accounting/Domain/Enums/SystemAccountPurpose.php:65-68`).

## 3. Domain model and schema contract

### 3.1 New columns and the DDL guard recipe (M7)

**Guard recipe (every migration of §11 step 1).** Each migration is written as four idempotent passes, so a rerun after ANY partial failure repairs exactly what is missing: (1) `ensureTable` — `Schema::hasTable` → `Schema::create` with plain columns only (uuid/decimal/string/boolean/timestamps; NO `constrained()`, NO `unique()` inside the closure); (2) `ensureColumns` — a declared `[column => definition]` list, `Schema::hasColumn` per column → `Schema::table` add; (3) `ensureConstraints` — every FK and CHECK is a NAMED constraint added by `ALTER TABLE <table> ADD CONSTRAINT <name> <definition>` only after `SELECT 1 FROM pg_constraint WHERE conname = ?` returns nothing (precedent `apps/api/database/migrations/tenant/2026_08_10_100000_add_inventory_valuation_mode_to_companies.php:43-52`; pgsql-only, as that precedent `:39-41`); (4) `ensureIndexes` — `CREATE [UNIQUE] INDEX IF NOT EXISTS` (precedent `apps/api/database/migrations/tenant/2026_04_24_000002_add_z_report_alert_type_unique_index_to_fraud_alerts.php:39`; SQLite honours `IF NOT EXISTS` too). Because FKs, CHECKs and indexes are never created inside `Schema::create`, the repair path IS the creation path. ### 4.4 Pattern-detection reference query (owner requirement; attribution default — orchestrator, owner to confirm, §12 OD-2)
Attribution rules: (1) per-line receipt events record exactly what each receiver posted (received/damaged, their identity, `actorRole = receiver`, blind flag); (2) a shortage discovered at close is a transfer-level fact — its line events carry `actorRole = closer` and are never counted as the closer's "mis-receiving" nor attributed to the last receiver; (3) the query is scoped per company and measures, per receiver, (a) damage reported vs the company baseline and (b) supervisor-confirmed variance on lines that receiver posted (receipt-line events joined to the close outcome on the same `transferLineId`); a line posted by several receivers counts for each of them with `shared_line = true`. Because `event_class` is indexed and `event_properties` is jsonb (`2025_11_30_102448_create_stored_events_table.php:16-17,20`), no new table is needed:
```sql
WITH e AS (SELECT event_properties AS p FROM stored_events
           WHERE event_class = 'App\Modules\Inventory\Domain\Events\StockTransferReceiptLineRecordedV1'
             AND created_at >= now() - interval '90 days'),
receipts AS (SELECT p->>'companyId' company_id, p->>'actorUserId' user_id, p->>'transferLineId' line_id,
                    (p->>'quantityDamaged')::numeric damaged FROM e WHERE p->>'actorRole' = 'receiver'),
closes AS (SELECT p->>'transferLineId' line_id, (p->>'quantityWrittenOff')::numeric written_off
           FROM e WHERE p->>'actorRole' = 'closer' AND (p->>'quantityWrittenOff')::numeric > 0),
baseline AS (SELECT company_id, AVG((damaged > 0)::int) damage_rate FROM receipts GROUP BY 1)
SELECT r.company_id, r.user_id, COUNT(*) lines_posted,
       AVG((r.damaged > 0)::int) damage_rate, b.damage_rate company_damage_rate,
       COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE c.line_id IS NOT NULL) lines_later_written_off,
       bool_or((SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT user_id) FROM receipts r2 WHERE r2.line_id = r.line_id) > 1) shared_line
FROM receipts r JOIN baseline b USING (company_id) LEFT JOIN closes c USING (line_id)
WHERE r.company_id = :company_id GROUP BY 1, 2, b.damage_rate ORDER BY lines_later_written_off DESC, damage_rate DESC;
```
T16 pins the shape. A T-4/later lane turns this into a read model + `fraud-alerts` rule.

### 6.4 Freight — exact default pending OQ-4 (M4 r1/r2; orchestrator default, owner to confirm)
**Today.** `capitalizeTransferCost` (`StockTransferService.php:631-707`) weights on shipped `quantity` (`computeAllocationWeights`, `:714-728`), allocates 100 % of `transfer_cost` with the last line absorbing the 4-dp residual (`:671-707`), and capitalises each share via `recordCostAdjustment` (`WeightedAverageCostService.php:745-831`), which writes a quantity-0 `Adjustment` movement (`:812-830`) and **no journal**, and no-ops when company-owned quantity is 0 (`:771-775`). Transfer freight has no GL leg in this module.

**Default algorithm (replaces the r2 "landed weights" text, which the gate showed cannot produce a residual).** At the terminal transition (`completed` or `closed_*`), once per transfer:
1. `goodTotal = Σ lines.quantity_received`, `sentTotal = Σ lines.quantity` (4-dp strings).
2. Working scale `w = max(ALLOCATION_SCALE, s + 4)` where `ALLOCATION_SCALE = 6` (`StockTransferService.php:65-70`) and `s = CurrencyScaleResolverInterface::getScaleSafe($company->currency_code, 3)` (rule 19; the terminal transition may run without `CompanyContext`).
3. `pool = CurrencyScale::bcformat(bcmul(transfer_cost, bcdiv(goodTotal, sentTotal, w), w), 4)` — one rounding, at the stored scale of `transfer_cost` (truncation, `docs/architecture/precision-contract.md:25-26`).
4. `freight_uncapitalized = bcsub(transfer_cost, pool, 4)` → `stock_transfers.freight_uncapitalized` (exact by construction; I7).
5. The existing allocation loop runs on `pool` instead of `transfer_cost`, with **landed weights**: `ProRataQuantity` → `quantity_received`; `ProRataValue` → `quantity_received × unit_cost_snapshot`; `EqualPerLine` → `1` for lines with `quantity_received > 0`, else `0`. Zero-weight lines are skipped (`:661-663`); the last cost-bearing line absorbs the residual so Σ `allocated_transfer_cost = pool` exactly (`:671-707`). `recordCostAdjustment` per line as today.
6. Damaged, written-off and returned units are all outside `goodTotal`, so their freight share is the residual: never capitalised, no journal, surfaced in reconciliation and carried on the header events.

**Worked example** (`transfer_cost = 120.0000`, `pro_rata_quantity`, TND `s = 3`, `w = 7`). Lines: A 8 sent, B 4 sent. Receipt: A 5 good, B 4 good; close `write_off` of A's remaining 3. `goodTotal = 9`, `sentTotal = 12`; `bcdiv('9','12',7) = 0.7500000`; `pool = bcformat(90.0000000, 4) = 90.0000`; `freight_uncapitalized = 120.0000 − 90.0000 = 30.0000`. Allocation on landed weights 5 and 4 (total 9): A `bcmul('90.0000', bcdiv('5','9',7), 7) = 49.9999995` → 4 dp `49.9999`; B (last) = `90.0000 − 49.9999 = 40.0001`. Persisted: `A.allocated_transfer_cost = 49.9999`, `B = 40.0001`, `freight_uncapitalized = 30.0000`; `49.9999 + 40.0001 + 30.0000 = 120.0000` (I7). Single-line T4 (12 sent, 5 good, 7 written off): `pool = 50.0000`, `freight_uncapitalized = 70.0000` = 7/12 of 120. T3 (11 good + 1 damaged of 12): `pool = 110.0000`, residual `10.0000`.

**Owner alternative (a) "expense with shrinkage"**: post the residual as Dr `InventoryShrinkageExpense` / Cr a transfer-freight clearing account. Requires a new `SystemAccountPurpose`, provisioning in `InventoryVarianceAccountProvisioner` and every seeded country chart, because freight was never debited to Inventory by this module (its invoice sits in Expenses/Purchasing) — a second P&L hit unless the clearing account is reconciled against that invoice. Not the default. Alternative (b) mode-consistent pool ratio (Σ landed weight ÷ Σ shipped weight instead of the quantity ratio) is listed for completeness; identical to the default under `pro_rata_quantity`.

Precision (rule 19): quantities numeric strings; `bccomp/bcadd/bcsub` at `QTY_SCALE = 4`; no float; `ForbidFloatCastOnDecimalProperty` guards models.
