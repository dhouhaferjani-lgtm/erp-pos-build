## Ranked Findings

### [BLOCKER] Existing products defaulting to `auto` will silently overwrite hand-set prices on the next WAC/cost event

Concrete failure scenario: before this feature, a merchant manually set product `P1.sale_price = 99.000` through the product update API. That is a legal existing flow: `sale_price` is validated in `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Presentation/Requests/UpdateProductRequest.php:163`, and `ProductController` mass-updates validated fields at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Presentation/Controllers/ProductController.php:689`. There is no current `pricing_mode` field in `Product::$fillable`; the product fillable list includes `sale_price` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:92` and margin overrides at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:110`, but no pricing mode through `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:117`. If the migration adds `products.pricing_mode default auto` exactly as proposed, `P1` becomes auto even though its price was historically hand-set. On the next purchase receipt, WAC writes a new `cost_price` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:281`, then calls `updateSalePrice()` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:288`. `updateSalePrice()` resolves target margin at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:186`, calculates the new sale price at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:189`, assigns it at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:202`, and saves at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:203`. The same clobber can occur on returns and cost adjustments via `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:580` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:762`.

Recommended change: do not backfill all existing rows to `auto`. Backfill `manual` for any existing product whose current `sale_price` does not equal the price computed from current `cost_price` and the currently effective target margin at the currency money scale; because there is no reliable historical intent column, the safer default for ambiguous rows is `manual` unless a deterministic equality check proves they were auto-priced. The migration should be explicit about using the current effective formula in `MarginService` and should be idempotent/auditable.

### [BLOCKER] Percent scale design contradicts the precision contract and existing product storage

Concrete failure scenario: category margins are proposed as `decimal(5,2)` with a 2-dp regex, but existing product margin override columns were widened to scale 3. The original product margin migration used scale 2 at `apps/api/database/migrations/tenant/2025_12_02_064541_add_cost_and_margin_fields_to_products_table.php:16` and `apps/api/database/migrations/tenant/2025_12_02_064541_add_cost_and_margin_fields_to_products_table.php:17`; the later widening migration moved them to scale 3 at `apps/api/database/migrations/tenant/2026_03_11_200000_widen_monetary_columns_to_scale_3.php:38` and `apps/api/database/migrations/tenant/2026_03_11_200000_widen_monetary_columns_to_scale_3.php:39`; the Eloquent casts still expose `decimal:3` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:151` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:152`. The precision contract says percentage fields use `regex:/^\d+(\.\d{1,2})?$/` and are fixed 2 dp, not currency-scaled, at `docs/architecture/precision-contract.md:36`. `MarginService` also returns effective margins at 2 dp at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:122` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:123`.

Concrete mismatch list:

- Proposed category migration: `decimal(5,2)` in the approach document line 37 matches the contract.
- Proposed validation regex: 2 dp in the approach document line 37 matches `docs/architecture/precision-contract.md:36`.
- Existing product DB: widened to `decimal(5,3)` at `apps/api/database/migrations/tenant/2026_03_11_200000_widen_monetary_columns_to_scale_3.php:38` and `apps/api/database/migrations/tenant/2026_03_11_200000_widen_monetary_columns_to_scale_3.php:39`, which does not match the percent contract.
- Existing product PHP model: `decimal:3` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:151` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:152`, which does not match the percent contract.
- Existing product DTO: passes raw strings without normalizing scale at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/DTOs/ProductData.php:86` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/DTOs/ProductData.php:87`.
- Existing FE atoms/settings: company settings use plain number inputs at `apps/web/src/features/settings/components/InventorySettings.tsx:203` and `apps/web/src/features/settings/components/InventorySettings.tsx:218`, with no visible 2-dp step or decimal-string component.

Recommended change: pick one percent scale and apply it across migrations, model casts, DTOs, validation, and FE inputs. The contract says 2 dp, so the spec should normalize percent fields to `decimal(5,2)` / `decimal:2` / 2-dp regex / string DTOs. If narrowing existing product columns from 3 dp to 2 dp, follow the documented pre-check requirement for narrowing at `docs/architecture/precision-contract.md:20` and abort when existing rows would truncate.

### [BLOCKER] Independent target/minimum inheritance can produce `minimum > target` and invert the margin bands

Concrete failure scenario: company default target is 30 and minimum is 15, consistent with current company fallback in `MarginService` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:111` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:117`. Now add category `A` with only `minimum_margin_override = 35`, and product `P` under `A` with only `target_margin_override = 25`. The proposed design resolves target and minimum independently. Result: `target=25` from product, `minimum=35` from category. A sale at 30% margin is above target but below minimum. Existing margin band logic checks minimum first at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:269`, returns orange below minimum at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:272`, and only checks target afterward at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:279`. That makes "above target" still a permission-sensitive below-minimum sale.

Recommended change: add an invariant that the resolved minimum cannot exceed the resolved target, and validate it at every write level that can affect resolution: company defaults, category overrides, product overrides, and any migration/backfill. The spec should define whether a partial override pair inherits as a pair from one level or independently; if independent resolution remains, the resolver must fail loudly or clamp by policy when the cross-level result violates `minimum <= target`.

### [BLOCKER] The proposed FE rewrite must eliminate all float money/percent arithmetic, not just change the formula

Concrete failure scenario: the approach says "Float-free via decimal.ts", but the current components that would be touched are not float-free. `PriceInputWithMargin` stores `value`, `costPrice`, `targetMargin`, and `minimumMargin` as numbers at `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:7`, `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:9`, `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:10`, and `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:11`; it uses gross-margin math at `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:22` and `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:27`; it parses user input with `parseFloat` at `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:142` and `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:167`; it rounds with native `Math.round` at `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:90` and `apps/web/src/components/molecules/PriceInputWithMargin/PriceInputWithMargin.tsx:111`. The precision contract forbids JS floats on money or quantity and directs frontend money arithmetic to Big.js helpers at `docs/architecture/precision-contract.md:53` and `docs/architecture/precision-contract.md:55`. The current inventory pricing card also uses `parseFloat` on `cost_price`, `list_price`, and margin overrides at `apps/web/src/features/inventory/components/pricing/ProductPricingCard.tsx:34`, `apps/web/src/features/inventory/components/pricing/ProductPricingCard.tsx:35`, `apps/web/src/features/inventory/components/pricing/ProductPricingCard.tsx:36`, and `apps/web/src/features/inventory/components/pricing/ProductPricingCard.tsx:37`.

Recommended change: make the spec require decimal strings end-to-end in the editable pricing UI. Replace number props and `parseFloat`/`Math.round` paths with `apps/web/src/lib/decimal.ts` helpers, whose API is string-based at `apps/web/src/lib/decimal.ts:49`, `apps/web/src/lib/decimal.ts:64`, `apps/web/src/lib/decimal.ts:79`, and `apps/web/src/lib/decimal.ts:94`. The FE acceptance criteria should explicitly reject `parseFloat`, `Number`, native arithmetic, and hardcoded 2-dp rounding in the touched pricing components.

### [SHOULD-FIX] Category ancestor resolution will create N+1 queries in product lists/reports unless the spec defines a batch resolver

Concrete failure scenario: `ProductController::index()` can paginate up to 2000 products at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Presentation/Controllers/ProductController.php:87`, eager-loads only the product's direct `category` relation at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Presentation/Controllers/ProductController.php:90`, and then maps every product to a DTO at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Presentation/Controllers/ProductController.php:131`. The proposed "reflect everywhere" requirement says list/report paths route through `getEffectiveMargins`. If that resolver calls `$product->category->getAncestors()` per product, `getAncestors()` executes a query at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Category.php:194` for each product with a non-root category. Inferred: a 2000-row product page can become 2000 additional ancestor queries before any report-specific aggregation.

Recommended change: require a batch category-margin resolver for list/report contexts. Acceptable designs include a single recursive CTE/materialized-path query per page/report, or preloading all ancestor categories for the distinct category paths on the page and resolving in memory. Do not let `getEffectiveMargins(Product)` hide per-row ancestor queries in high-cardinality contexts.

### [SHOULD-FIX] A single `source` and `source_category_id` cannot describe split target/minimum provenance

Concrete failure scenario: current `getMarginSource()` only looks at `target_margin_override` and then returns company, ignoring `minimum_margin_override`; see `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:360`, `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:362`, and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:372`. The proposed design keeps one `source` plus one `source_category_id`, while resolving target and minimum independently. If target comes from a product override and minimum comes from category `A`, `source='product'` and `source_category_id=null` hides the category-sourced minimum. If target comes from category `A` and minimum from category `B`, one `source_category_id` cannot represent both.

Recommended change: return per-field provenance, for example `target_source`, `target_source_category_id`, `minimum_source`, and `minimum_source_category_id`. If the UI still wants a summary source token, compute it from the richer data, not the other way around.

### [SHOULD-FIX] Pricing mode lacks a complete state machine and explicit reprice flows

Concrete failure scenario: the proposed rule says editing `sale_price` flips the product to manual, and editing a margin field re-arms auto. That misses non-form flows. Product imports/upserts set `sale_price` directly at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/ProductService.php:58`, and normal product updates mass-assign validated fields at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Presentation/Controllers/ProductController.php:689`. If those flows set `pricing_mode=manual`, WAC purchase/return/adjustment paths at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:288`, `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:580`, and `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:762` will stop repricing those products. If they do not set manual, they recreate the clobber bug from the first finding.

Recommended change: define an explicit state machine table for create, product form update, category inheritance change, company default change, import/upsert, bulk price update, WAC recalculation, and explicit "recalculate now". Include a deliberate command/API that can reprice manual products when the user asks for it, and make bulk/import behavior explicit instead of inheriting form semantics by accident.

### [SHOULD-FIX] Module-boundary risk: Inventory already depends on Product internals, and this design would deepen that coupling

Concrete failure scenario: repository guidance says cross-module communication must go through `Shared/Contracts/`, events, or a module's public service class at `CLAUDE.md:31`, and dependencies should be constructor-injected rather than resolved with `app()` at `CLAUDE.md:52`. The current WAC service constructor-injects `MarginService` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:44` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:45`, which is good for DI, but it also directly imports Product-module domain model `Product` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:18` and accepts `Product` objects in WAC methods at `apps/api/app/Modules/Inventory/Application/Services/WeightedAverageCostService.php:140`. The proposed hierarchy logic would make Inventory-triggered WAC recalculation depend on Product category traversal and Product pricing mode semantics.

Recommended change: put the repricing operation behind a Product-owned public service or `Shared/Contracts` interface that accepts stable identifiers/context and owns category/mode resolution internally. Do not make Inventory know category-margin hierarchy or pricing-mode details. Keep constructor injection; do not add `app()` or `resolve()` calls.

### [SHOULD-FIX] New mode/source strings need enum coverage, not scattered literals

Concrete failure scenario: the proposed `pricing_mode` column has literal values `auto|manual`, and `source` has literal values `product|category|company|default`. The repo already has domain enums for status/mode fields, including `App\Modules\Catalog\Domain\Enums\PricingMode` and `App\Modules\Workshop\Bundle\Domain\Enums\BundlePricingMode`; the proposed Product pricing mode is a different domain and should not reuse either. Existing Product model casts enum fields such as `type` and `enrichment_status` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:133` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:142`, but there is no `pricing_mode` cast in `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:130` to `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Domain/Product.php:157`. Current margin source is also a raw string returned from `getMarginSource()` at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:360` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Application/Services/MarginService.php:372`.

Recommended change: add `App\Modules\Product\Domain\Enums\PricingMode` and cast `Product::pricing_mode` to it. Also strongly consider an enum or DTO for margin source/provenance so `product/category/company/default` does not spread as magic strings across PHP and TypeScript.

### [NICE-TO-HAVE] Fiscal path appears safe, but the spec should explicitly preserve historical line immutability

Concrete failure scenario: I did not find evidence that product master `sale_price` rewrites existing signed SALE_RECEIPT bytes or immutable document lines. Product updates write the product row and emit a product event at `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Presentation/Controllers/ProductController.php:689` and `apps/api/app/Modules/Product/Presentation/Controllers/ProductController.php:695`; receipt lines persist the request line's `unit_price` at `apps/api/app/Modules/POS/Application/Services/ReceiptCreationService.php:231` and `apps/api/app/Modules/POS/Application/Services/ReceiptCreationService.php:340`; finalization computes and stores the fiscal hash at `apps/api/app/Modules/POS/Application/Services/ReceiptFinalizationService.php:97` and `apps/api/app/Modules/POS/Application/Services/ReceiptFinalizationService.php:105`. Product `sale_price` is read as a standalone current price in one composition helper at `apps/api/app/Modules/POS/Application/Services/ReceiptCreationService.php:1276`, which is future-facing rather than a historical rewrite.

Recommended change: keep the spec explicit that margin hierarchy changes only product master pricing and future price suggestions/defaults. It must not backfill receipt lines, document lines, canonical fiscal bytes, or fiscal hash-chain rows.
