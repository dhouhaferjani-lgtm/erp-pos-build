**Session:** L (wave-2 PO flow) · **Date:** 2026-09-01 · **Base commit:** `62964e5cc` · **Worktree:** `.worktrees/L-po-flow` (branch `test/L-po-flow`)
**Tenant profile under test:** fresh TN parapharmacy (IziPOS), currency **TND** (money scale **3**), quantity scale **4**.

## Scope

**In scope (wave 2):** PO create (both price-entry modes) → confirm → goods receipt (full / partial / over / under / batch+expiry lots / free quantities / price override / landed costs) → supplier invoice (create, link receipts, match, post) → supplier payment → the stock + GL + payable truth of every step. Settings woven in: units, tax rates (19 / 13 / 7 / 0 %), currency scale, supplier defaults.

**Out of scope (named by the brief):** POS (team tests manually) · imports & enrichment (wave 1, session K) · stock transfers (wave 3) · treasury beyond a supplier payment (wave 4) · accounting/declarations (wave 5) · deep RFQ (its own program; a `purchase_rfq → award → PO` smoke only) · supplier goods-return notes · OCR/DocumentIngestion (`SupplierInvoiceCommitter`) — unverified without an external provider, must not sit on the critical path.
