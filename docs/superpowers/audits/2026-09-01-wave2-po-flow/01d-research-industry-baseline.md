# Benchmark-first baseline — Purchase-to-Pay (PO → receipt → vendor bill → payment)

Reference systems: **Odoo 17/18** (Purchase, Inventory, Accounting) · **ERPNext v15** (Purchase Order, Purchase Receipt, Purchase Invoice, Payment Entry) · **Dolibarr 19/20** (Supplier orders, Receptions, Supplier invoices, Payments).

Cells marked **(from memory)** were not confirmed against an official doc page in this pass — treat them as claims to re-verify before they gate a decision. Cells with a source key (S1…S9) map to the source list at the bottom.

Scope of the flow benchmarked: PO draft → confirm → goods receipt (full / partial / over / under / lots+expiry / free qty) → supplier bill (link receipts, 3-way match, post) → supplier payment → stock + GL + payable truth.

## Edge cases the big three test for

These are the concrete scenarios our matrix should mirror, with the expected behaviour in each system.

1. **Bill attempted before any receipt, with Bill Control = Received quantities.** Odoo raises an explicit error and refuses to create the draft bill. ERPNext allows a Purchase Invoice against the PO but over-billing beyond the allowance blocks submit. Dolibarr allows it freely. → Our matrix must pin one behaviour per policy setting, not per user mood.
2. **Draft bill edited to a higher qty or a different price than received.** Odoo flags `Should Be Paid = Exception` and still allows posting (advisory). ERPNext blocks submit once the over-billing % is exceeded (blocking). Expect a deliberate choice; document it.
3. **Receive 110 when 100 were ordered, tolerance = 0.** ERPNext refuses (validation error) unless the user holds the "Role Allowed to Over Deliver/Receive". Odoo warns and permits. Dolibarr permits.
4. **Receive 110 when 100 ordered, tolerance = 10 %.** ERPNext accepts exactly up to 110 and refuses 111. Boundary test: 110.0000 accepted, 110.0001 refused.
5. **Three tranches of 30/30/40 against a PO of 100.** After tranche 2 the PO must read "partially received" with 60 received / 40 remaining; after tranche 3, "fully received" — computed from summed receipt lines, never from a stored flag (Dolibarr's issue #32528 is exactly a stale-flag bug).
6. **Receive 60 of 100 and close the PO.** The remaining 40 must disappear from incoming forecasts and from the "to receive" report, without any stock movement.
7. **Cancel a PO that has one submitted receipt.** ERPNext refuses until the receipt is cancelled; Odoo cancels the remaining picking and keeps the received part; nobody silently deletes stock.
8. **Cancel a submitted receipt.** Reversing stock ledger entries AND reversing GL entries (Stock ↔ GRNI) must appear; the original rows stay. Re-run the same cancel: it must be a no-op, not a double reversal.
9. **Tracked product received without a lot number.** Validation/submit fails with a field-level error before any stock moves.
10. **Tracked product received without an expiry date when the item has a shelf life.** The expiry auto-fills from the product's shelf-life days; a manual override is allowed; a blank expiry on an expiry-tracked item fails.
11. **Two receipts of the same product with different expiry dates, then an outgoing move under FEFO.** The earliest-expiring lot is consumed first; a lot already past its removal date is excluded from automatic selection.
12. **Free/bonus qty at price 0 under moving-average valuation.** Naive behaviour dilutes the average cost toward zero. Odoo/ERPNext let the user set the valuation rate independently of the purchase price — test that the WAC after a free receipt is what the accountant expects, not just "it posted".
13. **Receipt into a non-default warehouse when the PO named a different one.** Allowed with an explicit choice; the GL leg must hit *that warehouse's* stock account (ERPNext per-warehouse account), not the default.
14. **Landed cost applied after a partial receipt.** Only the stock from the referenced receipt is revalued; the not-yet-received part is untouched; unit cost of the on-hand qty rises by the allocated share.
15. **Landed cost applied after part of the received stock has already been sold/consumed.** The consumed share must go to COGS/expense (or trigger a repost), not silently inflate remaining stock. Both Odoo and ERPNext have a repost mechanism here; a naive implementation loses money.
16. **Price on the bill differs from the PO price (price variance).** Under perpetual valuation the difference goes to a price-difference account (or revalues remaining stock, per the costing method) — never left dangling in GRNI. Test that GRNI nets to zero for a fully received + fully billed PO.
17. **Partially received, fully billed PO.** GRNI must carry the correct one-sided residual and the residual must be explainable line by line — this is the report that catches everything else.
18. **Bill before receipt (invoice-first), then the receipt arrives.** The interim "not received" position must clear when the goods land; the net GL effect after both documents must equal the receive-then-bill ordering. Order-independence is the invariant.
19. **Mixed VAT rates plus a 0 % line on one bill.** The per-rate breakdown must show the 0 % base as a line with base > 0 and tax = 0; the sum of the bases must equal the net total exactly.
20. **TND (3 decimals) with per-line rounding.** Sum of 3-decimal line totals vs the document total computed from an un-rounded base — the two must reconcile, either by rounding per line consistently or via an explicit rounding-adjustment field (ERPNext's approach). Test a case engineered to drift: 3 lines × 0.3335.
21. **Foreign-currency bill paid at a different rate.** FX gain/loss is recognised on allocation; the payable clears to exactly zero in the bill currency and the residue lands in the FX account, not in the payable.
22. **Partial payment, then a second partial payment that overshoots the residual.** The excess becomes an unallocated supplier credit, usable against a later bill — it must never make an invoice's outstanding amount negative.
23. **Two concurrent payment allocations against the same invoice** (the ERPNext race, PR #32642). Both must not succeed in allocating the same residual — requires a row lock, not an application-level check.
24. **Advance payment against a PO before any bill exists**, then the bill arrives — the advance must be offerable for allocation and must not double-count as both an advance and an outstanding payable.
25. **Reverse a posted bill that is already partially paid.** Systems either refuse (must unallocate the payment first) or produce a credit note that leaves the payment on account. Whichever we pick, the payable balance must stay arithmetically true at every step.
26. **Repeat every one of the above in a second company and a second warehouse.** Numbering, supplier records, warehouse accounts and permissions must not bleed across (our own convention 09 "second-of-everything").
27. **A user with the receiving role only** attempts to confirm a PO and to post a bill: both must be refused at the server, not just hidden in the UI.
