**Counts:** 1 blocker / 5 high / 4 med / 1 low.

**Worst systemic issue:** The Document module is the structural weak point — `InvoiceController` (886 lines) carries fiscal create/post/convert orchestration that belongs in a service (SC-2), the Document Domain reaches sideways into other modules' Application services and runs raw SQL (SC-3, SC-4), and `DraftPersistenceService` float-multiplies price×quantity before persisting `line_total` (SC-1). Coupon/Promotion/Voucher/Loyalty are cleaner on layering but leak cross-module Domain coupling (SC-6) and route money through float in threshold paths (SC-7). Eloquent models at `Domain/` root are an accepted pattern and were not flagged.
