**Counts:** 4 BLOCKER, 4 HIGH, ~5 MED, 2 LOW (H9 dominates — 9 of them).

**Worst systemic issue:** Billing ships a correct bcmath `Money` VO but its entities (`Invoice`, `Payment`, `Refund`, `InvoiceItem`) bypass it and do raw `(float)` arithmetic with float-typed signatures that propagate through the controller/refund chain; float-equality status gates (Paid / fully-refunded) mean rounding drift can silently mis-state state. Secondary: critical fiscal logic (hash-chain engine, verify/backfill commands, GL listeners) and hardcoded bcmath-scale literals sit outside the Domain layer and outside the scale-resolver contract.
