**Summary:** The most urgent item is the float money bug in `AdvancedPaymentsModal.tsx:217` (canonical POS sale path). Systemically the data layer is leaky — settings/treasury/document pages bypass the `*Api.ts` convention and embed raw `api.*`/`fetch` + float money math in views (withholding + credit-note/treasury `types/` are the worst float offenders). Design-token adoption is the largest-by-volume debt but low-risk/mechanical. The team has internalized the API-unwrap and paginated-meta pitfalls (no real double-unwrap bugs), so F3 is essentially clean.
